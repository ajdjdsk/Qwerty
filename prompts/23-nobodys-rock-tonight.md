# Nobody's Rock Tonight (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Джаз-нуар торч-баллада + кинематографичный оркестр.

> **Смысл:** Женщина, которая для всех — «опора»: всех держит, всех спасает, всем нужна, и никогда не позволяет себе слабость. Сейчас два часа ночи, бар закрыт, она осталась последней — и наконец снимает маску силы, позволяя ночи (и музыке) подержать её саму. Песня о цене вечной силы и о праве иногда быть слабой. Новое направление: дымный джаз-нуар торч-сонг с кинематографичным размахом. Эмоция: усталая, интимная, щемящая → разрастается до большого катарсиса и снова стихает.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(an empty-bar hush — brushed drums, a walking bass, a smoky piano chord, a muted trumpet sighs, a lone violin answers)

[Verse 1 — smoky breathy chest, low and intimate, conversational]
The chairs are upside down, the night is almost through,
the bartender's pretending not to stare,
I've been everybody's harbor, everybody's "I'll get you,"
and tonight there's not a single soul to spare — for me.

[Pre-Chorus — voice opens, strings stir, swell begins]
'Cause I'm the one they call when it's caving in,
I'm the steady hand, the place where they begin —

[Chorus — soaring melismatic belt, violin and trumpet rising with the voice]
But I'm nobody's rock tonight,
no, I'm just a woman and a low-lit room,
let the music hold me, let it hold me tight,
let me lean on something other than my own perfume — of strength;
oh, lay me down, lay me down on a minor chord,
let me be the one who's caught, who's poured —
'cause I'm nobody's rock tonight.

[Verse 2 — fuller, the band leaning in]
I learned to swallow trembling, learned to keep my chin,
to be the calm that everybody borrows;
but a lighthouse gets so tired being battered by the wind,
and nobody asks the keeper of the sorrows — who keeps her.

[Pre-Chorus — bigger, organ underneath]
So pour me one more, let the saxophone confess
all the softness I have hidden in my dress —

[Chorus — larger, full noir strings and distant choir]
'Cause I'm nobody's rock tonight,
no, I'm just a heart in a borrowed coat,
let the strings come carry what I can't hold right,
let me float the way I let the others float — on me;
oh, set me down, set me down, let the brave girl rest,
let me be the held one, not the chest —
'cause I'm nobody's rock tonight.

[Violin & Muted Trumpet Solo]
(the two weave a smoky, aching duet over swelling strings)

[Bridge — drop to near-silence, voice and piano alone, raw]
And tomorrow I'll be granite, I'll be sure,
I'll be everybody's morning, everybody's cure —
but the clock says it's the hour no one sees,
so just for now... oh, just for now... let me be the one who's weak, who's freed —

[Final Chorus — colossal swell, full orchestra and choir, belt with grit, then receding]
'Cause I'm nobody's rock tonight,
and the whole tired room is finally on my side,
let it rise, let it rise, like a tide of light,
let me put the heavy, holy mask aside — at last;
oh, lay me down, lay me down on the softest sound,
let the night be strong so I can come unwound —
'cause I'm nobody's rock tonight.

[Outro]
(the orchestra recedes to brushed drums, a last muted-trumpet sigh and one warm held vocal note in the empty bar)
(nobody's rock... tonight...)
