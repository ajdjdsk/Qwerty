# The Sound I Swallowed (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Мощное ядро на максимум — эпик готик-симфо анфем, towering и катартический; приём «зажатые куплеты → извержение припевов», сквозной бессловесный крик от придушенного шёпота до боевого клича.

> **Смысл:** Сорок лет женщина делала себя тише, мягче, удобнее — спиливала каждый угол, что заставлял других морщиться, глотала каждое «нет» и каждую песню, отдавала свои «акры по дюйму». А теперь весь проглоченный за десятилетия звук извергается наружу. Песня о пожизненном самоумалении и об оглушительном, очищающем возвращении себя — права занимать место, звучать громко, быть всей собой; вес ему придаёт горе по годам, прожитым в сжатии. Близко каждому, кого приучили «быть поменьше». Эмоция: придушенная сдержанность → нарастающее давление → колоссальное, торжествующее извержение.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a lone tense violin, restrained; a held breath; a tiny choked hum — the swallowed sound, trying to get out)

[Verse 1 — constrained, quiet, swallowed, almost holding the throat]
For forty years I made myself a smaller, softer thing,
I filed down every edge that made them flinch;
I swallowed every "no," I swallowed every song to sing,
I gave away my acres, inch by inch.

[Pre-Chorus — pressure building, still held back]
but there's a sound inside a woman that you cannot keep,
it climbs from a whisper to a wildfire while she sleeps —

[Chorus — ERUPTION; colossal belt, full orchestra and choir]
so here's the sound I swallowed, here's the sound I swallowed whole,
forty years of "fine" and "sorry" coming up like molten gold;
I am done, I am done being palatable and small,
let it crack the careful ceiling, let it shake the dinner hall —
oh, here's the sound I swallowed, and it's louder than the shame,
and the whole wide world is gonna learn it — it's my name, it's my name.

[Verse 2 — the cost, the years; fuller but still tightening before release]
I clapped for every dream of theirs and quietly buried mine,
I called my disappearing being kind;
I was praised for how I vanished, I was loved for how I'd shine
the light on everyone but me — and oh, I'm done being blind.

[Pre-Chorus — bigger, the choir humming, the cry rising]
'cause the grief of all that shrinking is the fuel beneath the roar,
and I'm grieving her, that smaller me — and loving her — and MORE —

[Bridge — the grief under the power, then the biggest release]
(near-breaking, then unleashing)
this is not for getting even, this is not to make them pay,
this is just a woman taking up the space she gave away;
so I'm sorry, little quiet one, I'm sorry it took so long,
but I'm singing now for both of us — the silence and the song —
(the wordless cry erupts) — AHH —

[Violin & Orchestra Eruption]
(the violin screams skyward over the full thunderous orchestra and choir, the swallowed sound finally free)

[Final Chorus — maximum, key lifted, war-cry belt, choir and organ blazing]
so here's the sound I swallowed, here's the sound I swallowed whole,
and it's bursting every seam they sewed to keep me under control;
I will never be the smaller, never be the careful, small —
I'm the thunder in the rafters, I'm the writing on the wall —
oh, here's the sound I swallowed, and it's holy, and it's flame,
and the whole of me is roaring out at last — my name, MY NAME.

[Outro]
(the eruption settles into a vast, steady power; the violin holds one ringing, unbroken note, then a long calm breath)
(here's the sound... I swallowed... no more.)
