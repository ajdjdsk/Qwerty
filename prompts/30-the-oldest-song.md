# The Oldest Song (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Кинематографичная оркестровая колыбельная-баллада; один сквозной мотив через всю жизнь.

> **Смысл:** Одна простая колыбельная проходит сквозь всю жизнь, преображаясь: мать поёт её новорождённому; та же мелодия держит уже взрослую, испуганную женщину в больничных коридорах; потом она поёт её обратно своей умирающей матери — той, кто впервые её научил; и наконец — собственному младенцу. Любовь — это «самая старая песня»: она никогда по-настоящему не твоя, ты лишь держишь её недолго и передаёшь дальше по цепочке поколений. «Ты не первый, кто боится темноты, и не последний, кого подержат». Универсальная тема: любовь, смертность, нить, что связывает нас сквозь время. Эмоция: нежность над колыбелью → сокрушительная кульминация → тихий покой.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a solo violin hums a simple, aching lullaby motif; a music-box and celesta echo it; almost room silence)

[Verse 1 — the cradle; whispered tender chest voice, just music-box and violin]
Hush now, little fist against the night,
you came in screaming at the cold and bright,
but I have got you, oh, I've got you tight,
and I'll sing the only thing I know is right —

[Pre-Chorus — strings stir, voice warms]
the same few notes my mother used to weave
the nights I was too small to quite believe —

[Chorus — the lullaby blooms, soaring warm belt]
so hush now, hush now, you are held,
you are not the first to fear the dark and cold,
and you won't be the last to be told —
hush now, hush now, you are held;
it's the oldest song, and it was never really mine,
I just hold it for a while and pass it down the line,
so hush now, hush now... you are held.

[Verse 2 — decades later; the grown woman, alone and afraid, humming it to herself]
And the years came hard and the bed went cold,
and I hummed it to myself when I felt old,
in the parking lots, in the hospital halls,
that little tune still climbing up the walls —

[Pre-Chorus]
the same few notes, when there was no one near,
the only hands that ever held my fear —

[Chorus — fuller, strings swelling]
so hush now, hush now, you are held,
you are not the first to fear the dark and cold,
and you won't be the last to be told —
hush now, hush now, you are held;
it's the oldest song, and it was never really mine,
I just hold it for a while and pass it down the line,
so hush now, hush now... you are held.

[Bridge — at her mother's deathbed; role reversal; near-whisper, devastating, then rising]
And I stood where she once stood above my crib,
and I took the trembling hand that once took mine,
and I sang the whole thing back to where it lived,
to the woman who first taught me how to shine —
"hush now, mama, hush now, you are held,
you are not the first to walk into the light,
and I'll hum you, oh, I'll hum you through the night —"

[Violin & Orchestra Lift]
(the lullaby motif soars over the full orchestra, gathering a whole lifetime into one cresting wave)

[Final Chorus — now she sings it to her own newborn; key lift, full choir, belt with tears]
so hush now, hush now, you are held,
and one day you will sing it to your own,
to a fist against the dark, a brand-new bone —
hush now, hush now, you are held;
it's the oldest song, and it was never yours or mine,
we just hold it for a while and pass it down the line —
so hush now, hush now... you are held.

[Outro]
(everything falls away to the bare music-box and a single humming voice, the violin's last soft note)
(hush now... you are held...)
