# The Love Story Nobody Writes (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Доработанное ядро — готик-симфоническая кинематографичная баллада (тёплая, dark romantic), женский голос. Приём: маскировка под романтическую балладу с «раскрытием».

> **Смысл:** Главной любовью её жизни оказалась не романтика, а дружба — та подруга, что держала ей волосы в три ночи, мчалась через весь город по звонку, знала все её «версии» и оставалась рядом сорок лет. Песни пишут про влюблённых, а самую верную любовь — дружескую, «выбранную семью» — почти никто не воспевает; так что она воспоёт её сама. В бридже — лёгкая горько-сладкая нота: они уже немолоды, и кто-то уйдёт первым. Тема близка каждому, у кого есть такой друг. Эмоция: интимная нежность → удивлённое раскрытие → лучезарное, благодарное торжество дружбы.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a lone violin and piano, intimate and warm — sounding for all the world like the start of a love song)

[Verse 1 — tender chest voice, like a romantic confession]
You have seen me at my cruelest and my weakest and my best,
you've driven through the dark because I called;
you know the lies I tell myself, you've seen me undressed
of every bit of armor — you have seen it all.

[Pre-Chorus — strings stir, the warmth building]
and they'd assume a love like this was candlelight and rings,
but ours was something stranger, something truer than those things —

[Chorus — the reveal blooms, soaring warm belt]
'cause you are the love story nobody writes,
no, we never kissed, we never shared a name,
but you held my hand through forty years of nights,
and you'll be there to hold it when I'm leaving just the same;
oh, they sing about the lovers, but they'll never, never know
the truest one who stayed was the friend who wouldn't go —
you are the love story, the love story nobody writes.

[Verse 2 — the history, the showing-up; fuller, cello and harp]
you buried both my parents standing right beside my grief,
you talked me off a dozen kitchen floors;
you celebrated wins so small they'd fit upon a leaf,
and you loved the unloved corners no one saw.

[Pre-Chorus — bigger, french horn]
no vow, no aisle, no diamond ever made it true,
just a thousand ordinary days of me and you —

[Chorus — larger, radiant]
'cause you are the love story nobody writes,
no, we never kissed, we never shared a name,
but you held my hand through forty years of nights,
and you'll be there to hold it when I'm leaving just the same;
oh, they sing about the lovers, but they'll never, never know
the truest one who stayed was the friend who wouldn't go —
you are the love story, the love story nobody writes.

[Bridge — bittersweet; they're old now; near-whisper, then rising]
and we're greyer now, and slower, and we know how this must end,
that the universe will take one of us first;
so if I go before you, you remember, dearest friend,
you were not the second-best — you were the verse,
you were always, always the verse —

[Violin & Orchestra Lift]
(the violin and full orchestra surge, gathering a whole shared lifetime into one radiant wave)

[Final Chorus — key lift, full and luminous, choir, belt with happy tears]
so you are the love story, the love story they forgot,
the kind they never carve in any stone;
but I am carving you in everything I've got —
you're the warmest, bravest love that I have ever known;
let them sing about the lovers, I will sing of something more,
of the one who knew my whole heart and stayed standing at the door —
you are the love story, oh, the love story nobody writes.

[Outro]
(everything settles back to the lone violin and piano, intimate and warm)
(you were always... the love story...)
