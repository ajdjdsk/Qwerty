# Hallelujah in the Wreckage (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Готик-госпел-возрождение (gospel revival) + тёмный кинематографичный оркестр; 12/8 sway, call-and-response с хором.

> **Смысл:** Женщина приходит «в храм» с пустыми руками и растраченной верой, на самом дне — и оказывается поднята не потому, что жизнь вдруг наладилась, а вопреки руинам. Это песня о дерзкой благодати: воздеть разбитые руки и петь «аллилуйя» прямо посреди обломков — не как награду за счастливый конец, а как акт упрямого выбора подняться. Тема близка каждому, кто бывал на дне. Необычное направление. Эмоция: сломленная исповедь → экстатический, общинный, очищающий подъём.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a lone Hammond organ chord, a soft tambourine, a distant choir hum, a lone violin — intimate, like the last pew of an empty church)

[Verse 1 — intimate, gritty chest voice, nearly broken]
I came in with my hands all empty and my faith all spent,
no prayer left in the pocket of my coat;
I have buried what I worshipped, and I don't know where it went,
and I'm too tired to even clear my throat.

[Pre-Chorus — organ swells, choir hum rises, soft handclaps begin]
But something in the back row started humming sweet and low,
and it found the rusted hinges of my soul —

[Chorus — full gospel uplift, choir answering, belt with bluesy runs]
So it's hallelujah in the wreckage, hallelujah in the dark, (hallelujah!)
hallelujah for the broken who can't find the holy spark; (sing it!)
I'm not saved 'cause it got easy, I'm not healed 'cause it got light —
I just lift these ruined hands of mine and praise it anyway tonight;
oh, hallelujah, hallelujah, (oh yes!)
let the rubble be my choir and the ashes be my song —
hallelujah in the wreckage, and I'm singing right along.

[Verse 2 — fuller, handclaps and foot-stomps, tambourine driving]
I don't have the words for grateful, and I don't have reasons why,
the bills are still unpaid, the bed's still cold;
but a cracked and trembling "thank you" is a kind of learning to fly,
and a broke old hallelujah's worth its gold.

[Pre-Chorus — bigger, the whole room leaning in]
So if your knees are on the kitchen floor tonight,
you don't have to feel it — just begin —

[Chorus — larger, choir roaring back each line]
'Cause it's hallelujah in the wreckage, hallelujah in the dark, (hallelujah!)
hallelujah for the weary who are limping in the dark; (come on!)
you don't praise it 'cause you're winning, you praise it to survive,
you just lift those shaking hands of yours and choose to stay alive;
oh, hallelujah, hallelujah, (oh yes!)
let the heartbreak be the hymn and let the falling be the climb —
hallelujah in the wreckage, one more time, one more time.

[Bridge — drop to organ and bare voice, then the choir creeps back and lifts]
I used to think you had to mean it, had to feel the fire whole,
but grace don't check your paperwork before it grabs your soul;
so I'll fake it till I make it, I'll hum it till it's true,
and the singing, oh, the singing is the thing that carries through —

[Choir & Organ — Revival Vamp]
(key lifts a step; lead and gospel choir trade ecstatic ad-libs, claps and stomps driving, organ wailing)
(Choir: hallelujah, hallelujah — Lead: oh, anyway, anyway!)

[Final Chorus — colossal, key up again, full choir and orchestra, belt at the peak with grit]
So it's hallelujah in the wreckage, hallelujah in the storm, (hallelujah!)
hallelujah, I am standing, and I'm battered, and I'm warm; (yes I am!)
I was never saved by easy, I was saved by showing up,
by these stubborn, holy, ruined hands still lifting up the cup —
oh, hallelujah, hallelujah, (oh yes!)
let the wreckage be the altar where the broken ones belong —
hallelujah in the wreckage, and I'm singing, I'm still singing,
hallelujah, I'm still singing right along.

[Outro]
(the orchestra recedes to the lone organ and a single warm voice, tambourine softening)
(hallelujah... anyway... hallelujah... anyway...)
