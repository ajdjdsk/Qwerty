# Call the Sun Home (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** НОВОЕ НАПРАВЛЕНИЕ со всеми сильными сторонами ядра — нордический/скандинавский фолк-готик (никельхарпа, kulning, костяная флейта, рунический дрон) × полный готик-симфо оркестр и виртуозная скрипка. Приём: сквозной кулнинг-зов; рост от мёрзлой тишины к пылающему рассвету.

> **Смысл:** Полярная ночь — солнце не всходило два месяца, белое безмолвие и холод бесконечны. А женщина выходит на мёрзлый холм и зовёт солнце обратно, потому что бабка учила её: «когда ночь не кончается — не жди утра, иди и сотвори его». Песня о том, как пережить долгую тьму (депрессию, горе, тяжёлый сезон жизни), сохранить внутренний огонь и активно звать свет назад, а не пассивно ждать; и о вере, что свет всегда возвращается, как поворот колеса года. Близко каждому, кто проживал свою «зиму». Эмоция: мёрзлое одиночество тьмы → нарастающий зов и решимость → пылающий, торжествующий рассвет.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a vast frozen silence and wind; a lone ringing kulning call carries across it; a nyckelharpa drone and a bone flute enter beneath)

[Verse 1 — low, resonant, the long dark, restrained]
The sun went under sixty days ago and hasn't climbed,
and the white goes on forever, and the cold;
I have counted every candle, I have rationed every rhyme,
and the dark, the dark is patient, and it's bold.

[Pre-Chorus — frame drums begin, the call gathering]
but my grandmother, she taught me, when the night won't break,
you don't wait for the morning — you go out and you make —

[Chorus — the kulning soaring, nyckelharpa and violin rising, belt]
so I'll call the sun home, call it home, call it home,
I will stand upon the frozen hill and sing across the snow;
let my voice go out like firelight where the frightened fear to roam,
and I'll call until the embers in the sky begin to glow —
oh, I'll call the sun home, call it home, call it home,
'cause the light, the light has always, always come — I know, I know.

[Verse 2 — the inner fire; keeping the self alive through the dark; fuller]
there are winters of the body and there's winters of the soul,
and the longest ones don't show up on a map;
but I've banked a coal inside me that the frost will not control,
and I feed it with the calling, not the nap —

[Pre-Chorus — building, war-drum, runic drone deepening]
so let the cowards bar the shutters, let them sleep until the spring,
I will stand out in the teeth of it, and I will sing, I'll sing —

[Bridge — the faith; the wheel turns; the light is written, like the seasons]
it is older than my sorrow, it is surer than my fear:
the wheel will turn, the wheel will turn, the light has always come;
so I do not beg the heavens, I just call it loud and clear,
and I keep the ember burning till the frozen world goes numb — no more —

[Nyckelharpa, Kulning & Violin Sunrise-Build]
(the nyckelharpa, a soaring kulning call and the solo violin climb together as the first light cracks the horizon)

[Final Chorus — blazing sunrise, full orchestra and choir, kulning over the belt]
so I called the sun home, called it home, called it home,
and the rim of all the world is turning gold across the snow;
every soul that kept its ember, every voice that wouldn't roam,
we are standing in the daybreak that we sang up from below —
oh, I called the sun home, called it home, called it home,
and the light, the light has come again — I knew, I knew, it's home.

[Outro]
(the first warm light; the kulning softens to a tender call; wind, and one long resonant note over the thaw)
(call it home... call it home... it's home...)
