# Dust for a Name (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Спагетти-вестерн-готик — пустынный фронтир-score + тёмный кинематографичный оркестр.

> **Смысл:** Женщина оставляет позади прежнюю себя и похороненное прошлое и едет к неизвестному горизонту, где даже пыль не знает её старого имени. Песня о цене и свободе реинвенции — о том, как мучительно и одновременно очищающе оставлять того, кем ты был; и о том, что единственный призрак, который по-настоящему гонится за тобой, носит твоё собственное лицо. Она просит не прощения, а лишь дорогу и горизонт, достаточно большой, чтобы вместить всё. Новое направление. Эмоция: одинокая, пыльная решимость → широкий, почти искупительный простор.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(sparse and wide: a lone human whistle hook, a twangy tremolo-guitar sting, a distant wordless "aah", wind, then slow hoofbeat toms begin)

[Verse 1 — smoky, low, sparse, just guitar twang and whistle behind]
I left my name in a town that's three days dead behind,
nailed it to the door with the woman I let go;
the horizon doesn't ask me what I'm running from to find,
and the dust, the kind old dust, don't need to know.

[Pre-Chorus — the gallop builds, guitar swells, voice rising]
'Cause every mile is mercy when you've got a past like mine,
and the open road's the only church I've found —

[Chorus — sweeping, belt, wordless "aah" wails answering the lines]
So give me dust for a name, dust for a name,
let the wind take the woman that I was;
I am riding toward a morning that won't know me, won't reclaim,
won't whisper all the wrong that I have done — because
(aah-ah-ahhh) I'm not asking for forgiveness, just a road,
just a sky too wide to carry what I owed —
oh, give me dust for a name, dust for a name.

[Verse 2 — fuller, muted trumpet and strings enter, the gallop steady]
There's a grave back there with my good intentions in the ground,
and a dozen folks who'd spit to hear me well;
but you can't outrun a memory by putting towns around —
no, the only road that matters leads through hell.

[Pre-Chorus — building, harmonica cry]
So I'll make my peace at sundown with the things I cannot change,
and I'll ride into the burning, wide and proud —

[Whistle, Violin & Guitar Solo]
(the whistle theme returns and the violin and tremolo guitar trade lonesome lines over the gallop)

[Bridge — sudden quiet: just whistle, wind, and bare voice]
'Cause I've stared down every outlaw, every debt, every door,
and the only ghost still riding hard behind —
(the gallop creeps back in) — is the one I see in water, is the face I can't ignore,
so I'm racing my own shadow, and I think... I think I'm gaining ground.

[Final Chorus — epic horizon climax, wordless choir, full western orchestra, belt soaring]
So give me dust for a name, dust for a name,
let the desert wear the woman that I was;
I am riding for a country that won't shame me, won't reclaim,
won't hold me to the girl I had to lose — because
(aah-ah-ahhh) I'm not begging for an angel or a throne,
just a chance to ride out big and free and unknown —
oh, give me dust for a name, give me dust for a name.

[Outro]
(everything thins back to the lone whistle and wind, a final tremolo sting fading into the vast distance)
(dust for a name... dust for a name...)
