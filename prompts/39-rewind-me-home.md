# Rewind Me Home (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Экспериментальная ретроградная готик-арт-баллада — жизнь, рассказанная задом наперёд; обратные нарастания (reverse-свеллы), кульминация в «начале».

> **Смысл:** Умирающая женщина в тихом свете хосписа просит «перемотать» её жизнь назад — распустить седину и одинокие ночи, вернуть прощание обратно в дыхание, раз-прожить вдовство, материнство, первую любовь, юность, детство — до самого начала, в мамины руки, до всякой боли. Песня о примирении со всей прожитой жизнью и об универсальной, до боли знакомой тоске — вернуться к невинности и к тому, чтобы тебя просто держали. Эмоция: хрупкое старческое затихание → парадоксально нарастающий, светлеющий «обратный» подъём → один первый удар сердца и покой.

## Styles (≤ 1000)
Experimental cinematic gothic art-ballad told in reverse — a life rewinding from its end back to its beginning, with retrograde textures and a paradoxical build toward the start. Warm mature female mezzo-soprano / contralto, fragile aged near-whisper chest voice growing younger and brighter into a soaring melismatic belt, sob-catches, glides, breath, hush to wall of sound. A lead solo violin weaves continuously with the vocal; signature: reverse and backwards swells, pre-verb risers, reversed cymbals and backwards strings, a music-box and celesta, deep cello, harp, glowing piano, layered strings, distant choir, a soft heartbeat pulse that slows then quickens toward birth. Time seems to run backwards — decelerating yet swelling to a luminous climax at the beginning, then a single newborn heartbeat, minor into radiant major, film-score intensity, rich cathedral reverb, full symphonic production, ~70 BPM. ‑lo-fi, ‑EDM, ‑trap, ‑autotune, ‑male lead, ‑monotone

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a reverse swell rises out of silence, backwards strings and a reversed cymbal, a music-box, a slow heartbeat; a fragile aged voice)

[Verse 1 — the end; old age; near-whisper, frail]
So play me backwards, gently, from this quiet hospice light,
unwind the careful, folded, falling years;
take the silver from my temples, take the long and hollow nights,
unsay the last goodbye, give back the breath I wept in tears.

[Pre-Chorus — the reverse swells build, heartbeat steadying]
spool me past the empty kitchen, past the slow undoing,
let me feel his hand un-leave me, feel the front door soft un-close —

[Chorus — soaring, paradoxically lifting backward]
rewind me home, rewind me home,
past the widow and the warrior, past everything I've grown,
back before the breaking, back before I was alone —
oh, rewind me, rewind me, rewind me home.

[Verse 2 — un-doing the middle years; kids un-grown back into arms]
un-bury the small grave in springtime, un-cry it from my chest,
let the children shrink back laughing into my unburdened arms;
give me back the slammed-door fury, give me back the unmade bed,
and the wild, unbroken certainty that love would keep us warm.

[Pre-Chorus — bigger, brighter, the voice growing younger]
take me back behind the first grey hair, the first unspoken fear,
to a girl who hadn't learned yet how to fold —

[Chorus — fuller, radiant]
rewind me home, rewind me home,
past the lover and the leaving, past the woman fully grown,
back before the wanting, back before the world was stone —
oh, rewind me, rewind me, rewind me home.

[Bridge — youth, then childhood; decelerating yet swelling, luminous]
there's the dance, there's the first kiss un-kissing in the rain,
there's my mother in a doorway, young and whole;
the heartbeat's getting quicker now, the colours come again,
and the music, oh, the music's running home into my soul —

[Violin & Strings Reverse-Swell Lift]
(the violin and backwards-swelling strings climb to a luminous climax — the peak arriving at the very beginning of the life)

[Final Chorus — arrival at the cradle; key blossoms into radiant major, full choir]
so rewind me home, rewind me home,
to the very first of morning, to before I was my own,
to my mother's voice still humming the oldest song there's known —
oh, you rewound me, you rewound me, you rewound me home.

[Outro]
(everything resolves to a single newborn heartbeat and a mother softly humming a music-box melody, then a warm silence)
(rewind me... home... home...)
