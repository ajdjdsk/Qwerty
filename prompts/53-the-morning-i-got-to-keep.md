# The Morning I Got to Keep (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Доработанное ядро + небольшой улучшающий эксперимент — готик-симфо баллада-анфем; бессловесный хук в перекличке голос↔скрипка, «живое» исполнение, динамический дроп.

> **Смысл:** Год назад ей сказали, что она может не дожить, — а теперь она плачет над кофе от того, что обычный вторник всё-таки наступил. Песня о радикальной благодарности за обыкновенную жизнь после того, как чуть её не потеряла; о том, чтобы перестать «ходить во сне» и быть жадно живой к своей единственной жизни — даже к скуке, счетам и холоду. Каждый заурядный вдох — выигранное чудо. Близко выжившим, всем, кто пережил испуг, и каждому, кто выбирает быть благодарным. Эмоция: интимное узнавание чуда → нарастающая жадная радость → эйфоричное, живое торжество бытия.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a soft breath, then the wordless hook on a lone warm voice, answered by the solo violin)
(oh-oh-oh-ohh...) — (violin answers) — (oh-oh-oh-ohh...)

[Verse 1 — intimate, alive, close-mic, with little breaths]
I woke up to the kettle and the same old crooked light,
and I cried into my coffee like a fool,
'cause a year ago they told me I might not see this Tuesday quite —
and now the Tuesday's here, and oh, it's beautiful.

[Pre-Chorus — building, breathy, leaning in]
so I'm done, I am done with sleepwalking through the gift,
I want the cold, the bills, the boredom — all of it, all of it —

[Chorus — euphoric, the hook traded with the violin, soaring belt]
'cause this is the morning I got to keep, got to keep,
(oh-oh-oh-ohh) — (violin answers) — and I'm awake, awake;
let me feel it all, the silly and the small and the deep,
every ordinary, holy, lucky breath I get to take —
oh, this is the morning I got to keep, got to keep,
(oh-oh-oh-ohh) — and I'm alive, alive, alive, awake.

[Verse 2 — the catalogue of ordinary wonders, fuller, claps creeping in]
the neighbour's awful music and the dog that needs a walk,
the burnt toast and the laundry on the line;
I used to call them nothing, used to barely even talk —
now I'd kiss the dirty dishes, they're a sign that I've got time.

[Pre-Chorus — bigger, hand-claps, breaths]
so wake me, shake me, let me never get it numb,
let me ache with how I love it — here it comes, here it comes —

[Bridge — the DROP: stripped to bare voice, breaths and soft claps, intimate and raw]
(just voice and claps)
and if it's short, if it's short, then I'll burn it twice as bright,
I will not waste one ordinary, glittering, given night —
(a laugh, a breath) — so come on, come on, hit me with the whole of it again —

[Violin & Voice Hook Duet]
(the voice and the solo violin trade the wordless hook, then soar it together as the band crashes back in)

[Final Chorus — key lift, euphoric, choir and claps, belt with joy]
'cause this is the morning I got to keep, got to keep,
(oh-oh-oh-ohh) — (violin soaring with her) — and I'm awake, awake;
let me love it loud and clumsy, let me love it hard and deep,
every ridiculous and beautiful and lucky breath I take —
oh, this is the morning I got to keep, got to keep,
(oh-oh-oh-ohh) — and I'm alive, alive, alive — awake.

[Outro]
(the wordless hook one last time, voice and violin together, then a soft laugh and a breath)
(oh-oh-oh-ohh... I got to keep it... I got to keep it...)
