# Dance the Dark Away (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Итало/евро-диско конца 1970-х × готик-симфо ядро (вдохновлено колоритом La Bionda — лейбл не упоминается в самом промпте). Four-on-the-floor, роскошные струнные, тёплый аналоговый звук, «crying on the dancefloor».

> **Смысл:** Женщина приходит на летнюю неоновую дискотеку с разбитым сердцем — но вместо того чтобы плакать в углу, она вытанцовывает боль из себя. Свет, бит и роскошные струнные становятся катарсисом: грусть превращается в движение, а движение — в горько-сладкое самовозвращение и свободу. Тема близка каждому, кто лечил сердце танцем и музыкой. Эмоция: меланхоличный готик-плач → эйфоричный, но щемящий диско-восторг → светлое, повзрослевшее затихание.

Warm mature female mezzo-soprano / contralto, soulful chest voice to a soaring melismatic belt, late-70s warmth, expressive glides and breath, intimate to euphoric. A lead solo violin weaves continuously with the vocal, playing both sweeping virtuosic disco-string lines and an aching lament over the groove; a four-on-the-floor kick, syncopated funky disco bass, lush cascading string section, warm analog arpeggiated synth, clavinet and wah guitar, brass stabs, shimmering hi-hats, handclaps, tambourine, pipe organ and choir at the peak. ## Lyrics

[Intro — rubato gothic lament, no beat yet]
(a lone violin and piano, slow and aching; she stands at the edge of the dancefloor, heartbroken)
The mirror ball is bleeding little diamonds on the floor...

[Verse 1 — still melancholy, the pulse only hinted]
and your seat is empty, like it's been since you walked out,
I almost turned around tonight and slipped back through the door,
but the bassline caught me, caught me — turned my sorrow inside out.

[Pre-Chorus — the four-on-the-floor kick and bass drop in, building]
'Cause I'm not gonna cry in the corner of the night,
no, I'm gonna let the rhythm do the crying — so watch me —

[Chorus — full disco euphoria, sweeping violin and strings, soulful belt]
so I dance, dance, dance the dark away,
let the summer and the neon take the ache,
I will move until the morning, till there's nothing left to say,
till the floor forgets your name and my poor heart learns how to break — free;
oh, I dance, dance, dance the dark away,
and these lights, these lights are kinder than the day —
so I dance the dark, I dance the dark away.

[Verse 2 — full groove, lush strings cascading]
There are strangers' hands like sunlight, there's a beat just like a pulse,
there's a version of me laughing that I thought I'd left for dead,
and I don't need you to find me, I don't need you to absolve —
I've got three hundred heartbeats and a mirror ball instead.

[Pre-Chorus — building, brass stabs]
'Cause the sweetest kind of vengeance is a woman who is free,
spinning gold out of the heartbreak — come and watch me —

[Chorus — bigger]
so I dance, dance, dance the dark away,
let the summer and the neon take the ache,
I will move until the morning, till there's nothing left to say,
till the floor forgets your name and my poor heart learns how to break — free;
oh, I dance, dance, dance the dark away,
and these lights, these lights are kinder than the day —
so I dance the dark, I dance the dark away.

[Bridge — the beat strips away; the lone violin lament returns for a breath; raw]
(the grief sneaks back in the quiet)
And yes, I'll feel it later, when the lights come up too bright,
when the taxi's full of silence and the city's gone to grey...
(the four-on-the-floor crashes back, euphoric) — but that is for the morning,
'cause tonight, oh, tonight — I dance the dark away!

[Violin & Strings Disco Solo]
(the violin tears through a virtuosic sweeping disco line over the relentless groove, strings cascading)

[Final Chorus — key lift, euphoric peak, choir and organ]
so I dance, dance, dance the dark away,
I am laughing, I am crying, I am gloriously astray,
I will move until I'm lighter, till I've given grief away,
till I'm nobody's leaving — I'm just here, I'm here to stay — alive;
oh, I dance, dance, dance the dark away,
and the dawn, the dawn can have me when it may —
but I danced, I danced, I danced the dark away.

[Outro — the groove fades back to the lone violin lament, lighter now, almost smiling]
(I danced... the dark away...)
