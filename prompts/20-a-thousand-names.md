# A Thousand Names (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Мистический восточный/суфийский оркестровый транс (модальный).

> **Смысл:** Душа всю жизнь странствует — по соли, пеплу и снегу — в поисках того, кого не может назвать: дома, Бога, любви, покоя. Ей говорят, что искомого не найти, но она понимает: само томление, сам поиск и есть молитва; а то, что она ищет, носит тысячу имён и едино. В кульминации она перестаёт спрашивать «как тебя звать» и просто растворяется в звуке — ответ не где-то, а вокруг. Мистическое, древнее, экстатическое — суфийское кружение и сдача. Свежий восточный саунд, не похожий на прошлые треки.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a low drone hums, a lone duduk breathes a modal call, a frame-drum heartbeat and soft hand percussion begin)

[Verse 1]
I have walked the salt and the cinder and the snow,
asking every stranger if they'd seen your face,
I have prayed in a hundred tongues I didn't know,
to a door, to a wind, to an empty, holy place.

[Pre-Chorus]
(the drone swells, strings enter, the pulse quickens)
And they told me, "child, you'll never come to find it,"
but the looking, oh, the looking is the prayer —

[Chorus]
'Cause you've got a thousand names,
and I have called you every one,
you're the home and you're the going,
you're the dark behind the sun;
oh, I am the wick and you're the flame,
and the burning is the same —
beloved, beloved, whatever you are called,
I surrender to your thousand names.

[Verse 2]
(fuller, the trance deepening, oud and dulcimer shimmering)
I built my whole religion out of restlessness and ache,
I made a shrine of every road I'd roam,
and the more I tried to name you, oh, the more I came awake
to the ache itself becoming, slowly, home.

[Pre-Chorus]
(percussion accelerating, claps driving)
So I stopped my desperate asking, let the longing be the altar,
and I felt the whole horizon start to spin —

[Bridge]
(whirling like a dervish, the drums racing, the voice spinning into wordless modal ecstasy)
(la-la-lai... ah-ah-ahhh... ya-lai-lai...)
I stopped asking what to call you, I just spun into the sound,
and the answer wasn't anywhere — the answer was around.

[Duduk & Violin Trance Solo]
(the duduk and modal violin chase each other, accelerating, ecstatic)

[Final Chorus]
(full ecstatic peak, soaring choir, whirling percussion)
'Cause you've got a thousand names,
and tonight I'll sing them all,
you're the falling and the rising,
you're the answer and the call;
oh, I am the wick and you're the flame,
and the burning is the same —
beloved, beloved, whatever you are called,
I am yours beyond your thousand names.

[Outro]
(the whirl slows back to the lone drone and duduk, one final breath, then silence)
(a thousand names... a thousand names...)
