# Barefoot in the Storm (Opus 4.8)

> **Смысл:** После долгих месяцев оцепенения — когда жила «на автомате», как призрак в собственной кухне — женщина в разгар грозы распахивает дверь, скидывает туфли и выбегает танцевать под ливень. Это не безрассудство, а возвращение к жизни: к телу, к чувствам, к ярости и радости быть живой. Дождь смывает онемение. Отклонение по настроению: экстатическое, дикое, эйфоричное, дерзко-радостное. Максимально «живое» — со смехом, вскриками и нарастающим вихрем.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a lone fiddle kicks in fast and alive over a hand-stomp and claps, distant thunder, then a breathless whoop)

[Verse 1]
I unlatched the door at the height of it,
the sky was cracking open like a drum,
they all said "stay inside, you'll catch your death" —
but oh, I think my death had already come.
'cause I've been a ghost in my own kitchen,
six grey months of going through the rote,
so I kicked my good shoes in the gutter
and I let the thunder grab me by the throat.

[Pre-Chorus]
And the rain came down like a hallelujah,
cold and holy, running down my spine —

[Chorus]
So I'm barefoot in the storm,
got the mud between my toes and I'm alive,
I am soaking, I am roaring,
and I've never felt so wildly, fully mine —
let it pour, oh, let it pour,
let it wash the heavy numb right off my skin;
I'm barefoot in the storm,
and I'm letting all the living back in.

[Verse 2]
There's a woman in me I'd forgotten,
the one who used to dance until she fell,
who laughed too loud and loved too sudden,
who never learned to do the careful, well —
and she's spinning in the downpour with me,
two halves of one drowned, delighted soul,
and the lightning's like a chorus saying
"there you are — oh, there you are — you're whole."

[Pre-Chorus]
And the rain came down like a hallelujah,
washing off the years I held my breath —

[Chorus]
So I'm barefoot in the storm,
got the mud between my toes and I'm alive,
I am soaking, I am roaring,
and I've never felt so wildly, fully mine —
let it pour, oh, let it pour,
let it wash the heavy numb right off my skin;
I'm barefoot in the storm,
and I'm letting all the living back in.

[Bridge]
(near-whisper, breathless, just fiddle and a heartbeat drum, then it explodes back)
I don't need it to be pretty,
I don't need it to make sense,
I just need to feel the cold, the wet,
the reckless, holy, here-and-now intense —
(oh-oh) so come on, sky, you give me everything you've got,
'cause I am done, I'm done, pretending that I'm not —

[Fiddle & Hurdy-Gurdy Solo]
(whirling, ecstatic, accelerating, stomps and claps driving)

[Final Chorus]
(euphoric, whoops and a full choir, the orchestra blazing)
'Cause I'm barefoot in the storm,
and the whole sky's pouring blessings on my head,
I am laughing, I am crying,
I am everything they swore that I was not, instead —
let it pour, oh, let it pour,
till there's nothing left but living on my skin;
I'm barefoot in the storm,
and oh, the storm — the storm just let me win.

[Outro]
(the band drops to rain and one warm, laughing held note, fiddle fading into the downpour)
(let it pour... let it pour...)
