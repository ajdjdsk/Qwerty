# The Women Before Me (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** НОВОЕ НАПРАВЛЕНИЕ + ЭКСПЕРИМЕНТ — балканско-болгарские «mystery voices» (диссонансный открытогорлый женский хор) × готик-симфо ядро; асимметричный размер (7/8→9/8), кластеры-диссонансы, схлопывающиеся в унисон.

> **Смысл:** Женщина слышит хор всех женщин, что были до неё — матери, бабок, безымянной линии тех, кто хоронил сыновей, поднимал каменистую землю, пел, «чтобы не закричать», и выживал. Она — живой край этой несгибаемой линии, и она никогда не одна: их сила в её крови, их заглушённые голоса поднимаются в ней. Песня о женской родовой стойкости, о наследии и принадлежности, о возвращении голоса тем, кого молчали поколениями. Тема близка каждому, кто черпает силу в своих корнях. Эмоция: одинокая, земная решимость → нарастающий хор предков → яростный, объединённый триумф голоса.

## Styles (≤ 1000)
Balkan / Bulgarian "mystery voices" folk fused with cinematic gothic orchestra — primal, ancient, fierce and haunting, experimental and striking. Warm mature female mezzo-soprano / contralto lead, rich open-throated chest voice with sharp ornaments, microtonal glides, melismatic, breath, intimate to towering. Signature experiment: an open-throated dissonant Balkan women's choir in tight microtonal close-cluster harmony (the ancestral voices) answering the lead, the clusters finally resolving into one unison at the peak; an asymmetric meter (7/8 then 9/8) driving hypnotically; a drone bed, kaval flute, gadulka and tambura, a lead solo violin weaving continuously with the vocal, deep cello, frame drums and tapan, dulcimer, layered strings. Minor modal harmony with a raised-fourth bite, a slow build from a lone voice to a vast clan-chorus, film-score intensity, rich reverb, full symphonic-folk production, ~92 BPM. ‑lo-fi, ‑EDM, ‑trap, ‑autotune, ‑male lead, ‑monotone

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a lone modal drone; a kaval flute keens; a single open-throated voice cries out; a frame drum begins an off-kilter 7/8 pulse)

[Verse 1 — fierce, grounded, ornamented chest voice]
I came up out of women who were never on a page,
who buried sons and broke the rocky ground,
who sang to keep from screaming, who grew iron out of rage,
and I feel them in my marrow, in the low and ancient sound.

[Pre-Chorus — the choir begins to gather in dissonant clusters]
can you hear them, can you hear them, in the cluster and the cry,
all the throats they tried to silence rising up in me tonight —

[Chorus — the "mystery voices" choir blooms in dissonant close harmony, the lead soaring above]
'cause the women before me, they sing in me, sing in me,
a hundred crowded voices in a harmony of stone;
I am not the first to suffer, I'm not the last to be set free,
and I never, never, never have to do it on my own —
oh, the women before me, they rise in me, rise in me,
and the song that they were strangled with is roaring to be known.

[Verse 2 — the long line; grandmothers, the unnamed, the survivors; meter shifts to 9/8]
there's the one who crossed an ocean with a baby and a name,
there's the one who learned to read by candle, hid the book;
there's the one who never married, who the village put to shame,
and they're standing all behind me with the strength it took.

[Pre-Chorus — bigger, the clusters thickening, gadulka and violin keening]
every "no" they ever swallowed, every dream they had to drown,
is a brick inside the woman who is now refusing down —

[Bridge — the dissonant clusters tighten to their sharpest, then RESOLVE into one blazing unison]
(the voices grind into dissonance, almost unbearable, then snap into a single note)
so take the cluster and the discord, all the centuries of ache,
and let it find one note at last, one voice the dark can't break —
(the unison rings) — we are one throat now, we are one,
and the silence, oh, the silence is undone —

[Kaval, Gadulka & Violin Dance]
(the kaval, gadulka and solo violin spin an asymmetric, accelerating folk dance over the tapan drums)

[Final Chorus — vast clan-chorus, the choir and lead in towering unison, then radiant harmony]
'cause the women before me, they sing in me, sing in me,
and I am the living edge of an unbroken, ancient line;
what they couldn't finish, I will carry till I'm free,
and the daughters after me will feel me in their spine —
oh, the women before me, we sing as one, as one,
and the song they tried to bury is the brightest in the sun.

[Outro]
(everything falls back to the lone drone and the single open-throated voice — now haloed by a faint choir-echo that never quite leaves)
(they sing in me... they sing in me...)
