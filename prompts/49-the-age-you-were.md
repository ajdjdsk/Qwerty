# The Age You Were (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Доработанное ядро — готик-симфоническая кинематографичная баллада (интимная, dark romantic), женский голос. Приём: смена тональности минор→мажор в момент прозрения.

> **Смысл:** Немолодая женщина находит старую фотографию матери, где той столько же лет, сколько дочери сейчас, — и вдруг видит не всесильную фигуру, которую годами винила, а испуганную, измотанную молодую женщину, делавшую что могла со своими ранами. Многолетние обиды смягчаются в позднее, щемящее прощение. Мать уже ушла, и прощение приходит слишком поздно, чтобы сказать вслух, — но освобождает дочь от наследованной горечи. О примирении с несовершенным родителем и о том, как взросление учит состраданию. Близко почти каждому. Эмоция: холодная обида → прозрение → тёплое освобождающее прощение и любовь.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a lone violin and piano, restrained and a little cool, distant; a faint clock, the rustle of old paper)

[Verse 1 — clipped, restrained, the old grievances, cool minor]
I found you in a shoebox, twenty-nine and holding me,
a cigarette, a tired smile, a kitchen I forgot;
for years I kept a ledger of the ways you failed to be
the mother on the magazines — and God, I judged a lot.

[Pre-Chorus — strings stir, still guarded]
I blamed you for the slamming doors, the love you couldn't show,
for the soft words that you swallowed — but there's something I didn't know —

[Chorus — the key PIVOTS into warm major; soaring, forgiving belt]
'cause now I'm the age you were, the age you were back then,
and I'm frightened and I'm flailing and I'm doing what I can;
I can see a scared young woman where I used to see your sin,
and I'd give the world to tell you, mama — that I understand —
oh, I'm the age you were, and the anger's turning kind,
I am walking in your worn-out shoes, and they're heavier than I knew.

[Verse 2 — warmer, understanding deepening, cello and harp]
you were carrying your own ghosts, your own mother's heavy hand,
you were younger than my children when you learned to bury pain;
and the things I swore I'd never do, I've done — and now I understand
that you weren't the storm, you were a woman standing out there in the rain.

[Pre-Chorus — building, french horn, tender]
so I'm setting down the ledger, I am tearing up the score,
'cause the girl who wrote it never knew what being grown was for —

[Bridge — bittersweet; she's gone now; the forgiveness comes too late to say aloud, but frees]
and I know you cannot hear me, that I left it far too long,
that the saying comes in shoeboxes and not across a room;
but I forgive you, oh, I forgive you — and I forgive me being wrong,
and I'll carry you so gently now, no anger, just the bloom —

[Violin & Orchestra Lift]
(the violin and full orchestra rise warm and major, a whole reconciliation cresting)

[Final Chorus — radiant tender major, key lifted, release with tears]
'cause I'm the age you were, and at last, at last I see,
not a villain, not a saint, but just a heart that tried its best;
and the love I couldn't reach you with, it's flooding back through me,
and I'll mother my own gently now, and let your ghost finally rest —
oh, I was the age you were, and the anger turned to grace,
and I'd give the whole world over just to hold your tired face.

[Outro]
(the orchestra settles to the lone violin and piano; the photograph set down gently, warm)
(the age you were... I understand... I understand...)
