# I'd Choose the Fall (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Грандиозная кинематографичная симфо, ardent/luminous (мажор).

> **Смысл:** Зрелая женщина, которую жизнь уже ранила, знает наизусть «арифметику разбитого» — и всё равно выбирает любить полностью, без брони, зная, что можно снова упасть. Потому что осторожная полу-жизнь за стенами — и есть настоящая смерть. Песня о смелости любить (и вообще жить) на полную после боли. Намеренное ОТКЛОНЕНИЕ по настроению от предыдущих: не скорбь и не ярость, а тёплое, страстное, светлое, парящее «да» жизни. Тот же готический оркестр, но мажорно-лучезарный.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(warm rising violin over glowing piano and a harp glissando)

[Verse 1]
I know the arithmetic of broken things,
I've counted every way a heart can crack,
I've read the weather that a goodbye brings —
and still, oh still, I'm never looking back.

[Pre-Chorus]
'Cause the safest rooms are just a gentler kind of grave,
and I didn't claw back out of all that dark to play it safe —

[Chorus]
So I'd choose the fall, I'd choose it all,
the terrifying, beautiful, unguarded yes;
I would rather love you and risk the fall
than live so walled I never have to guess.
let the careful build their careful little halls —
I'm running headlong toward the fire in my chest,
oh, I'd choose you, I'd choose you, I'd choose the fall,
'cause a half-lived life's the only real death.

[Verse 2]
They taught me caution like it was a crown,
to ration out my wonder, drop by drop,
but I have spent enough of my one life lying down —
and I am done, oh, done, with playing it to stop.

[Pre-Chorus]
So if the cost of feeling is the chance of being broken,
then break me open, break me wide — let the whole of me be spoken —

[Chorus]
So I'd choose the fall, I'd choose it all,

I would rather love you and risk the fall

[Bridge]
(quieter, intimate, then lifting)
And maybe it ends — oh, maybe it does,
maybe I'll stand in the rubble once more;
but I'd rather have burned for the thing that it was
than be tidy and cold and unsure.
(oh-oh) so take my reckless, wide-awake, unarmored heart —
this is the bravest thing I'll ever do —

[Violin Cadenza]

[Final Chorus]
(key lift, full radiant orchestra, choir)
So I choose the fall, I choose it all,
the dizzy, holy, fully-living yes;
I would rather rise and love and risk the fall
than die in all that safe and careful "less."
let the frightened keep their fortress and their walls —
I am throwing wide the windows of my chest,
oh, I choose it, yes, I choose it, I choose the fall,
and the falling, oh, the falling is the best.

[Outro]
(strings glow down to the warm violin and a single bright held note, dissolving into light)
(I'd choose it all... I'd choose the fall...)
