# A Choir of One (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Эксперимент ядра — готик-арт-баллада как вокальный канон/раунд; один голос множится в кафедральный хор из себя самой и снова стихает до одного.

> **Смысл:** Женщина осталась одна — тишина пустого дома в восемь вечера, одна чашка, один прибор — и боится этой тишины. Пока однажды не открывает рот, чтобы разбить молчание, и не обнаруживает, что может наполнить дом собой: её голос возвращается эхом, и она гармонизирует сама с собой. Одиночество превращается в священную самодостаточность: «быть одной» — не значит «быть одинокой»; я сама себе компания, хор из одного человека. Тема близка всем, кто живёт один или учится быть с собой. Эмоция: страх пустоты → удивлённое раскрытие → полнота и покой.

Experimental cinematic gothic art-ballad built as a vocal canon / round — one voice multiplying into a self-harmonized cathedral choir, then back to one at peace. Warm mature female mezzo-soprano / contralto, intimate dry close chest voice opening to a soaring melismatic belt, layered in canon and counterpoint against delayed copies of herself (close harmonies, rounds, overlapping echoes stacking into a vast choir of the same voice); sob-catches, glides, breath, lone-room hush to wall of sound. A lead solo violin also canons against its own double, weaving continuously with the vocal; deep cello, harp, glowing piano, celesta, layered strings, pipe organ swelling at the peak. The arrangement builds company out of solitude — one lonely voice to a full self-made chorus then resolving to a single peaceful voice, minor into warm major, film-score intensity, rich cathedral reverb, full symphonic production, ~72 BPM. ‑lo-fi, ‑EDM, ‑trap, ‑autotune, ‑male lead, ‑monotone

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a single dry, close voice humming in an empty room; a lone violin; the hum begins to echo and answer itself in canon)

[Verse 1 — alone, the dread of the silence; intimate, dry, close]
The house gets loud with nothing when the door clicks shut at eight,
just the kettle and the clock and the careful sound of me;
I used to fill the quiet with a voice across the plate,
now there's one cup softly steaming, only one set place to be.

[Pre-Chorus — her voice starts to layer; a second copy answers a beat behind]
but I opened up my mouth tonight just to break the spell,
and the strangest comfort came back round — my echo, doing well —

[Chorus — the canon blooms; she harmonizes with her own returning voice, a choir of herself]
so I am a choir of one, a choir of one,
I will sing into the silence till the silence comes undone;
every note I send out lonely comes back round to me in kind,
and I harmonize with no one but the woman left behind —
oh, I am a choir of one, a choir of one,
and the empty, holy chapel of my life is not so done.

[Verse 2 — the layers thicken; she fills the house with herself, rounds overlapping]
I set two cups this morning and I laughed and put one back,
then I sang the dishes clean in a round, a three-part me;
turns out the company I waited for was never what I lacked —
it was sitting in my own throat, waiting patiently.

[Pre-Chorus — building, more voices stacking in counterpoint]
so I'll be my own soprano and my own low steady ground,
I will be the congregation and the bell and all the sound —

[Bridge — the turn; from lonely to chosen; a vast cathedral of her own overlapping voices]
there's a difference, oh, there's a difference, that took me years to learn:
between the cold of being lonely and the warm of being alone;
one is a wound that's waiting, one's a fire you tend and earn —
and tonight, with all my voices, I am finally, fully home.

[Violin & Vocal Canon]
(the lead voice and its layered copies, and the violin against its own double, weave a full soaring round at its richest)

[Final Chorus — full self-made cathedral choir, sacred, radiant, then beginning to thin]
'cause I am a choir of one, a choir of one,
and I filled my own cathedral when I thought that I had none;
I don't need another's harmony to know my song is whole,
I'm the singer and the silence and the chapel and the soul —
oh, I am a choir of one, a choir of one,
and the quiet isn't empty — it's a stage, and I've begun.

[Outro]
(the layered voices fall away one by one until a single dry, close voice remains — humming, unhurried, at peace, no longer lonely)
(a choir... of one... and that's enough...)
