# Carry Them Home (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Сакральный реквием — литургический оркестрово-хоровой (лат. хор).

> **Смысл:** Реквием не отчаяния, а благословения: женщина наконец откладывает многолетнее горе по всем, кого потеряла, — не забывая их, а «провожая домой» в покой и отпуская себя вместе с ними. Латинский хор (requiem, lux, dona eis pacem) сплетается с английскими куплетами как обряд прощания-благословения. Песня о том, как скорбь превращается в благодать, а память — в свет. Новое направление: сакральный литургический реквием, кафедральный размах. Эмоция: тяжёлая скорбь → возвышенное освобождение и покой.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a single tolling funeral bell, a low organ drone, a distant Latin choir breathes, a lone violin enters like a candle being lit)
(Choir, soft Latin: Requiem... lux aeterna...)

[Verse 1 — reverent, hushed chest voice, long sustained vowels]
I have carried you in pockets sewn of years,
your names like stones I never set back down,
I built a quiet country out of tears
and wore my grief the way some wear a crown.

[Pre-Chorus — strings stir, organ swells, choir hum rises]
But the dead don't need my breaking to be loved,
and you never asked me, darling, not to mend —

[Chorus — soaring melismatic belt, choir answering in Latin]
So I carry them home, I carry them home,
to the light, to the long, kind rest,
I'm not letting go — I am laying down the stone,
I am blessing every name I've pressed against my chest;
go gently, go gently into morning,
I will love you from the living, not the grave —
(Choir: Dona eis pacem...)
oh, I carry them home, I carry them home.

[Verse 2 — fuller, processional, cello and bell deepening]
I thought that if I stopped the aching, you would fade,
that the grief was the last rope between us two;
but grief was just the love with nowhere left to wade,
so I'll pour it into light instead of you.

[Pre-Chorus — bigger, organ and timpani building]
For the ones who stayed and the ones who slipped away,
let me turn this heavy sorrow into prayer —

[Chorus — larger, full choir, high ethereal descant]
So I carry them home, I carry them home,
to the light, to the long, kind rest,
I'm not letting go — I am laying down the stone,
I am blessing every name I've pressed against my chest;
go gently, go gently into morning,
I will love you from the living, not the grave —
(Choir: Lux aeterna, dona eis pacem...)
oh, I carry them home, I carry them home.

[Violin & Organ Interlude]
(the violin soars over the full pipe organ and Latin choir, a sacred cresting wave)

[Bridge — sudden hush, voice almost a whispered prayer, then rising]
And when it is my turn to be the name,
the candle and the bell, the quiet stone,
I pray some gentle voice will do the same —
will lift me, will forgive me, carry me back home...
(Choir, swelling: In paradisum...)

[Final Chorus — radiant climax, minor blooming to major, full choir and descant, belt with tears]
So carry me home, carry them home,
into light, into long, kind rest,
we are not letting go — we are laying down the stone,
we are blessing every soul we ever pressed against our chest;
go gently, go gently into glory,
you were loved, you were loved, you were brave —
(Choir: Dona eis pacem, requiem...)
oh, carry them home, carry them home.

[Outro]
(the choir and organ resolve to a single warm major chord, the lone violin and one last bell, then peaceful silence)
(carry them home... carry them home...)
