# The Floor Will Remember (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Фламенко-готик (duende) — фламенко + кинематографичный оркестр.

> **Смысл:** Героине всю жизнь твердили: горюй тихо, прилично, не занимай места, будь маленькой. Но её боль не умеет шептать — и вместо того чтобы её прятать, она вбивает её в пол танцем, позволяя телу сказать невыразимое и превращая скорбь в огонь, гордость и достоинство. «Пол запомнит то, чего никогда не покажет моё лицо». Тема близка многим: право не уменьшать себя и выразить то, что не выговорить словами. Новое направление — фламенко-готик с «duende». Эмоция: сдержанная боль → яростная, гордая, очищающая стихия.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a lone flamenco guitar rasgueado, a slow palmas clap, a distant "ay", a violin answers)

[Verse 1 — smoky, restrained chest voice, just guitar and soft palmas]
They told me grieve it quiet, keep it pretty, keep it small,
fold your trouble in a handkerchief and hide it from them all;
but my sorrow doesn't whisper, no, my sorrow wants the room,
so I will not sit and shrink — I'll let it bloom.

[Pre-Chorus — palmas build, cajon enters, voice rising with a quejio edge]
'Cause the words won't ever hold it, and the tears are not enough,
so I give it to my heels, and I give it to the love —

[Chorus — full stamping compas, fierce belt with melismatic quejio cries]
So let the floor remember what my face will never show,
let it thunder, let it thunder everything I can't let go;
I will stamp it, I will stamp it till the silence has to break,
I'm a grief become a fire — for my own, my own sake;
ay, let them watch me, let them see me turn to flame,
I am no one's quiet sorrow, I am no one's gentle shame —
let the floor remember, let the floor remember my name.

[Verse 2 — fuller, dramatic strings and castanets enter, the compas insistent]
You can take the voice, the letters, take the years I can't reclaim,
but you cannot take the language that is older than your name;
when the heart has more than mouths can hold, the body learns to speak,
and oh, my body's roaring what they told me was too weak.

[Pre-Chorus 2 — bigger, brass stabs, organ underneath]
So I pour the unforgiven and the wanted and the lost
into every burning beat — and I will pay the cost —

[Guitar & Violin Duel]
(fiery picado guitar and the violin trade blistering runs, accelerating)

[Bridge — breakdown: ALL orchestra cuts out; only palmas, zapateado foot-stomps and a bare, raw voice]
(just claps, heels, and breath)
No strings to hide behind now, no soft and pretty lie,
just the pounding and the woman and the truth she can't deny —
one, two — and the heels reply, one, two — and the heart won't break,
(the stomps gather, faster) now everybody, for my sake —

[Final Chorus — the full orchestra and choir crash back, colossal, belt at the peak, "ole!"]
So let the floor remember what the world refused to know,
let it thunder, let it thunder all the fire I didn't show;
I have stamped it, I have stamped it, and the silence finally broke,
I'm a grief become a glory — and I'm rising through the smoke;
ay, now let them tell me to be quiet, to be tame,
I am no one's gentle shadow, I am no one's gentle shame —
let the floor remember, let the floor remember my name.

[Outro]
(the orchestra falls away to the lone guitar, a final "ay", one last heel-stamp, then silence)
(let the floor... remember my name...)
