# Through Me Like a Storm (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** ЭКСПЕРИМЕНТ С МУЖСКИМ ГОЛОСОМ (временно) — душевный блюз-госпел-готик × кинематографичный оркестр; дымчатый баритон с хрипотцой, взлетающий в щемящий фальцет (в духе того мужского тембра, что тебе нравится — исполнитель в промпте не упоминается).

> **Смысл:** Закрытый, «огороженный» мужчина, привыкший держать всё под контролем, оказывается сметён чувством больше себя — всепоглощающей любовью, что срывает крышу с его обороны. «Ничего больше не важно»: броня и мелкое испуганное «я» опадают, и он впервые по-настоящему живой. Песня о капитуляции, о том, как тебя ломает и пересобирает буря чувства, об ужасе и экстазе потери контроля. Близко каждому, кого однажды захлёстывало с головой. Эмоция: настороженное затишье → нарастающий шторм → воющая, освобождающая капитуляция.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a distant thunder rumble, a lone slide-guitar moan, low gospel organ, a violin; hushed and still)

[Verse 1 — smoky soulful baritone, intimate, low]
I was a quiet country, I was fields all neat and fenced,
I drew my borders careful, built my walls;
then you crossed my still horizon like a pressure dropping tense,
and I felt the first cold warning of it all.

[Pre-Chorus — the surge approaches, organ swelling]
and it's coming, oh, it's coming, I can taste it on the air,
all my careful, guarded order — I don't care, I don't care —

[Chorus — the surge breaks, baritone howling up into falsetto]
'cause it moves through me like a storm, like a storm,
and there's nothing, there is nothing left to keep;
let it tear the roof off everything that kept me lukewarm,
let it flood me, let it take me, let it run me to the deep —
oh, it walks through me like weather, and I'm done being warm,
so come and move through me, move through me like a storm.

[Verse 2 — fuller, slide guitar and stomps, the body of the storm]
I spent my whole life dry and safe and starving for the rain,
calling it my wisdom, calling it my peace;
now the thunder's in my chest and I would do it all again,
'cause a man who's never flooded never feels release.

[Pre-Chorus — building hard, gospel choir humming]
let it break me like a levee, let it leave me on my knees,
I would rather drown in feeling than be careful, than be these —

[Bridge — the EYE of the storm; sudden hush; intimate, tender falsetto, almost silence]
(everything goes still)
and right here in the center, where it's quiet, where it's wide,
I can see you, I can see you, and there's nowhere left to hide;
nothing matters, nothing matters but the eye of all of this —
just your face inside the silence, and the trembling of a kiss —

[Slide Guitar & Violin Storm-Build]
(the slide guitar and violin wail and climb as the back of the storm comes roaring in)

[Final Chorus — full tempest, howling falsetto climax, gospel choir, full orchestra]
'cause it moves through me like a storm, like a storm,
and I'm letting go of everything I am;
let it flood the quiet country, let it leave me newly born,
let it take the careful man and give me back a louder man —
oh, it walks through me like weather, and I'll never be reformed,
so move through me, move through me, move through me like a storm.

[Outro]
(the storm passes; distant thunder receding, a calm slide-guitar sigh, organ, one long breath)
(move through me... like a storm...)
