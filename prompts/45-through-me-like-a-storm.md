# Through Me Like a Storm (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** ЭКСПЕРИМЕНТ С МУЖСКИМ ГОЛОСОМ (временно) — мощный мелодичный хард-рок × кинематографичный готик-оркестр; гриттовый анфемный мужской вокал (тёплые низы → надрывный белт с хрипотцой), сигнатурный дрон диджериду (в духе вокала/группы, что ты назвал; исполнитель в промпте не упоминается).

> **Смысл:** Закрытый, «огороженный» мужчина, привыкший держать всё под жёстким контролем, оказывается сметён силой больше себя — бурей чувства/судьбы, что срывает крышу с его обороны. Он перестаёт сопротивляться и сам становится этой бурей. «Ничего больше не важно»: броня и мелкое испуганное «я» опадают, остаётся только мощь и нагота правды. О капитуляции, что оборачивается силой, об ужасе и экстазе потери контроля. Эмоция: настороженное затишье → нарастающий шторм → героическая, воющая капитуляция-триумф.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a distant thunder rumble, a low didgeridoo drone, a lone clean guitar line and a violin; hushed and still, the calm before)

[Verse 1 — controlled, warm gritty baritone, intimate and low, holding back the force]
I was a quiet country, I was fields all neat and fenced,
I drew my borders careful, built my walls;
then it crossed my still horizon like a pressure dropping tense,
and I felt the first cold warning of it all.

[Pre-Chorus — guitars and drone swelling, the surge approaching]
and it's coming, oh, it's coming, I can taste it on the air,
all my careful, guarded order — I don't care, I don't care —

[Chorus — the surge breaks: a huge soaring rasping rock belt, ringing high]
'cause it moves through me like a storm, like a storm,
and there's nothing, there is nothing left to keep;
let it tear the roof off everything that kept me lukewarm,
let it flood me, let it take me, let it run me to the deep —
oh, it walks through me like weather, and I'm done being warm,
so come and move through me, move through me like a storm.

[Verse 2 — fuller, driving guitars and drums, didgeridoo pulsing, the body of the storm]
I spent my whole life dry and safe and starving for the rain,
calling it my wisdom, calling it my peace;
now the thunder's in my chest and I would do it all again,
'cause a man who's never broken never feels release.

[Pre-Chorus — building hard, choir humming, drums pounding]
let it break me like a levee, let it leave me on my knees,
I would rather drown in living than be careful, than be these —

[Bridge — the EYE of the storm; sudden hush; a clear ringing voice over the lone drone, almost fragile]
(everything goes still)
and right here in the center, where it's quiet, where it's wide,
I can see it, I can feel it, and there's nowhere left to hide;
nothing matters, nothing matters but the eye of all of this —
just the truth without the armor, and the terror, and the bliss —

[Guitar & Violin Storm-Build]
(the guitar and violin wail and climb as the back of the storm comes roaring in over the didgeridoo)

[Final Chorus — full tempest, howling rasping rock belt with a ringing high cry, choir, full orchestra]
'cause it moves through me like a storm, like a storm,
and I'm letting go of everything I am;
let it flood the quiet country, let it leave me newly born,
let it take the careful man and give me back the louder man —
oh, it walks through me like weather, and I'll never be reformed,
so move through me, move through me, move through me like a storm.

[Outro]
(the storm passes; distant thunder receding, the didgeridoo drone and a calm clean-guitar sigh, one long breath)
(move through me... like a storm...)
