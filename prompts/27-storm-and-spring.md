# Storm and Spring (Opus 4.8)

> **Смысл:** Прожив много «зим и вёсен», героиня перестала прятаться от бурь — теперь она распахивает ставни навстречу грозе, потому что знает секрет: любая буря проходит и становится весной, и именно она делает наступившее затишье таким зелёным и сладким. Песня о том, чтобы принять весь цикл жизни — и тёмные сезоны тоже, — и встречать тяжёлое с раскинутыми руками. Тема близка каждому. Новое направление — барочный концерт в духе Вивальди. Эмоция: бурное ликование → нежное затишье → триумфальное обновление.

## Lyrics

[Intro — Allegro]
(driving harpsichord continuo and a fast, fiery baroque violin ritornello; crisp terraced dynamics — sudden forte, sudden piano; storm energy from the first second)

[Verse 1 — Allegro, fast and agile, lightly ornamented vocal]
Here it comes again, the sky goes iron-grey,
the same old thunder calling out my name,
I used to run and bolt the doors and pray it all away —
now I throw the shutters open to the rain.

[Pre-Chorus — terraced swell, voice lifting]
For I have counted forty winters, forty springs,
and I know, oh, I know the secret that it brings —

[Chorus — the ritornello returns under the voice, belt with baroque runs]
So let it storm, let it storm, let it break across the land,
let the heavens do their worst and let it pour,
I am not afraid of weather, I have learned to understand
that the wildest, darkest tempest is a door;
let it turn, let it turn, let it thunder into spring,
for the winter never won a single thing —
so let it storm, let it storm... then let me sing.

[Verse 2 — Allegro]
They think the brave ones never tremble in the gale,
but the brave just learned the gale will always pass,
that the cruelest, coldest season ever written into tale
still dissolves like frost upon the morning grass.

[Pre-Chorus — building]
So I'll dance where once I cowered, I'll be glad,
for the storm is half the beauty that I had —

[Chorus — bigger, ritornello driving]
So let it storm, let it storm, let it break across the land,
let the heavens do their worst and let it pour,
I am not afraid of weather, I have learned to understand
that the wildest, darkest tempest is a door;
let it turn, let it turn, let it thunder into spring,
for the winter never won a single thing —
so let it storm, let it storm... then let me sing.

[Largo — slow tender middle movement]
(the storm breaks; harpsichord and lute soften, an oboe d'amore sighs, the violin turns tender and slow; intimate vocal)
And oh, the hush that follows, the green and dripping calm,
the way the whole world glistens, washed and new;
I would not know this stillness, would not feel this balm,
if I'd never, never let the tempest through.

[Violin Cadenza & Ritornello — Allegro rebuild]
(the baroque violin blazes a virtuosic cadenza, the ritornello gathers, the storm energy returns triumphant)

[Final Chorus — Allegro, triumphant, full baroque ensemble and a small chorus]
So let it storm, let it storm, let it shake me to the root,
I will meet it with my arms flung open wide,
for I am every season, I'm the blossom and the fruit,
I'm the winter and the spring they can't divide;
let it turn, let it turn, let it thunder into light,
I have made my peace with every day and night —
so let it storm, let it storm... and then I'll rise.

[Outro]
(the ritornello resolves, harpsichord and a final warm violin flourish, one last bright chord)
(let it storm... then comes the spring...)
