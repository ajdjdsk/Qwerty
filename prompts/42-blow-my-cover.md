# Blow My Cover (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Нео-нуар «шпионский» / femme-fatale оркестр × готик-симфо ядро; сёрф-нуар твэнг-гитара, медные стэбы, вибрафон, опасный гламур.

> **Смысл:** Женщина в совершенстве владеет искусством быть непознаваемой — обаятельная, холодная, «та, что уходит первой», никого не пускающая за фасад; у неё «легенда поверх легенды». Но цена в том, что она спряталась так хорошо, что потеряла саму себя, — и самое одинокое не «попасться», а никогда не быть по-настоящему узнанной. Когда кто-то смотрит на неё так, будто читает её как досье, которое она считала сожжённым, она решает «сдать себя»: позволить увидеть настоящую, рискнув быть раненой. Песня об эмоциональной броне и смелости близости. Близко всем, кто прячется за стенами. Эмоция: холодный гламурный контроль → трещина и обнажённость → смелое решение быть увиденной.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(the spy theme: a twangy reverb guitar lick, a sharp brass stab, a cool vibraphone shimmer, a sultry pizzicato walking line — dangerous and composed)

[Verse 1 — smoky, controlled, sly chest voice]
I am very good at exits, I'm an artist of the door,
I'll be charming, I'll be smoke, and then I'm gone;
I keep my secrets in a safe behind a smile you'll just adore,
and nobody, no, nobody gets the combination on.

[Pre-Chorus — pizzicato tightening, brass muttering]
I've got a cover for the cover, got a story for the truth,
I could pass a lie detector on my own heart in the booth —

[Chorus — sultry belt, the spy theme driving underneath]
'cause I never blow my cover, never blow my cover, no,
I'm the one who leaves the party while you're guessing at my name;
I've gone undercover, undercover, oh, so long I don't know
which of all these clever women is the one beneath the game —
no, I never blow my cover, never let the mask drop low.

[Verse 2 — the cost surfacing, fuller noir strings]
I have charmed a hundred rooms and walked out colder than I came,
I've got alibis for feelings I won't claim;
but it's lonely in the safehouse, it is quiet behind the frame,
and the woman in the mirror's just a passport with no name.

[Pre-Chorus — tension, the strings leaning in]
'cause the danger was never getting caught, my dear, it's this:
that I'd vanish so completely there'd be no one left to miss —

[Bridge — the cover CRACKS: the noir cool dissolves into exposed, tender, naked orchestra; near-whisper]
(the groove falls away)
and then you looked at me too long, and something in me turned,
like you were reading me, oh, like a file I thought I'd burned;
and I felt the safehouse trembling, felt the cover start to slip,
and for once I didn't run — I let you see the trembling lip...

[Violin & Brass]
(the spy theme returns, but wavering, unsure, then steadying with a new warmth)

[Final Chorus — the choice; sultry but cracking open, belt with raw heart]
so blow my cover, blow my cover — God, just blow it wide,
I am tired of being clever, I am tired of being gone;
take me undercover, undercover, where there's nowhere left to hide,
and I'll trade a hundred exits for one door I don't walk on —
oh, come and blow my cover, blow my cover... let me be... alive.

[Outro]
(the spy theme plays once more, softer now, the guitar twang warm and unguarded, fading into the dark)
(blow my cover... let me be known...)
