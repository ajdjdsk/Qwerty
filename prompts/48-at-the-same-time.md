# At the Same Time (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Эксперимент ядра — политональность (мажор + минор одновременно): светлая мажорная мелодия поверх скорбной минорной гармонии, два тональных центра, срастающиеся в один аккорд.

> **Смысл:** Мать на свадьбе дочери переполнена счастьем за неё — и в тот же самый миг горюет: нет её собственной матери, чтобы это увидеть; её брак давно распался; маленькая девочка, которую она растила, теперь ушла во взрослую жизнь. Оба чувства абсолютно истинны и не отменяют друг друга. Поворот: она перестаёт выбирать одно из двух и позволяет правде быть двойной. Песня о зрелой мудрости — что самые большие моменты жизни редко бывают «одним чувством», и о смелости держать радость и скорбь вместе, не делая ни одну ложью. Близко каждому, кто переживал светлое и горькое одновременно. Эмоция: смятение двойного чувства → принятие → примирённая, полная правда.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a bright major piano-and-violin line and a grieving minor cello line play at the same time, gently dissonant; then a single voice over both)

[Verse 1 — tender, the voice moving between the bright and the dark]
I'm watching you in white across a floor I can't believe,
and my face is doing something it can't name;
half my heart's a rising sun, the other half a grieve,
and the band is somehow playing both the same.

[Pre-Chorus — the two tonalities pulling]
'cause your grandmother's not here, love, and your father and I are through,
but you're radiant, oh, you're radiant, and I made it here for you —

[Chorus — soaring, the major and minor entwined under the belt]
so it's joy and it's mourning at the same time, same time,
it's the brightest and the heaviest my heart has ever known;
I am laughing, I am breaking, on the very same dime, same dime,
and I'm done, oh, I'm done pretending I can pick just one to own —
let it be the major and the minor, intertwined, entwined,
let me feel the whole impossible of it, at the same time.

[Verse 2 — fuller, the two lines weaving harder, harp and cello]
they want a tidy feeling, want a single thing to say,
"are you happy?" — yes — "are you sad?" — oh, that as well;
I have learned the heart's a room where opposites can stay,
and the truest moments ring like a cracked but holy bell.

[Pre-Chorus — building, both keys swelling]
so I won't choose the sunrise and pretend I lost no light,
I will hold the whole of all of it, the wrong one and the right —

[Bridge — the reconciliation; she stops choosing; the two keys begin to resolve toward one]
maybe grown-up isn't picking, maybe grown-up's holding both,
the wedding and the funeral in one trembling breath;
so I'll dance with all my ghosts tonight and honour every oath,
and I'll let the joy be deeper for its nearness to the death —

[Violin & Orchestra — the two melodies interlock]
(the bright major violin line and the minor cello lament finally weave into one, climbing together)

[Final Chorus — the bitonality resolves into a warm major chord with a tender minor shadow, radiant]
so it's joy and it's mourning at the same time, same time,

it's the fullest that a heart can hold, sublime and unsublime,
it's the whole of being human shining wet behind my eyes —
let it be the major and the minor, reconciled, aligned,
oh, the truest love is all of it, at the same time.

[Outro]
(the chord rings — major, with one held minor note glowing inside it — then a lone violin, at peace)
(at the same time... at the same time...)
