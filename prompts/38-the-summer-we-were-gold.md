# The Summer We Were Gold (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Тёплое средиземноморское «закатное» итало-поп/диско конца 1970-х × готик-симфо ядро (золотая сторона колорита La Bionda — лейбл в самом промпте не упоминается). Нейлоновая гитара, Rhodes, lush-струнные, мягкий грув.

> **Смысл:** Спустя годы женщина находит выцветшую фотографию и вспоминает одно «золотое» лето — мимолётную любовь, которая определила целый сезон её жизни. Она вспоминает не с горечью, а с теплом и мудростью: то лето было прекрасным именно потому, что не могло длиться; золото на то и золото, что не вечно. «Нельзя погасить сезон, что уже закатился тебе в самые кости». Тема близка каждому, у кого было своё незабываемое лето/первая любовь. Эмоция: тихая выцветшая ностальгия → золотой, полнокровный расцвет памяти → тёплое, повзрослевшее принятие.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(present day, intimate and faded: a warm nylon acoustic guitar and a lone violin, soft, a little wistful)

[Verse 1 — present-day reflection, close and tender]
I found the photograph today, the one the sun got into,
your arm around a girl I used to be;
the colour's almost gone now, but I swear I still can feel the blue
of that water, and the heat of you and me.

[Pre-Chorus — the groove gently blooms in, the memory waking to full colour]
and it all comes back in technicolour — the salt, the cheap guitar —
oh, we didn't know, we didn't know how golden that we are —

[Chorus — full golden Italo groove, violin and Rhodes trading, soulful belt]
'cause the summer we were gold, the summer we were gold,
we burned the whole of August like we'd never, never grow old;
and I know it had to end, I know the autumn took the throne,
but you can't un-shine a season that has set into your bones —
oh, the summer we were gold, the summer we were gold.

[Verse 2 — the memory in full colour, lush strings, vibraphone]
We had a borrowed scooter and a transistor by the sea,
a hundred lire and the whole of everything;
you couldn't sing to save your life, but you'd still sing it loud to me,
and the evenings tasted of the songs we'd bring.

[Pre-Chorus — building, brass and handclaps]
and they tell you to forget it, that it's foolish to look back,
but the gold, the gold is not a wound — it's all the light I packed —

[Chorus — bigger]
'cause the summer we were gold, the summer we were gold,
we burned the whole of August like we'd never, never grow old;
and I know it had to end, I know the autumn took the throne,
but you can't un-shine a season that has set into your bones —
oh, the summer we were gold, the summer we were gold.

[Bridge — the wisdom; groove eases, then surges back]
and maybe that's the secret no one tells you when you're young:
that gold does not last — that's why it's gold;
it was perfect 'cause it ended, it was holy 'cause it's gone,
and I carry that whole summer when I'm cold —

[Violin & Rhodes Solo]
(the violin and warm Rhodes piano trade a sun-drenched, soaring line over the groove)

[Final Chorus — key lift, full warm climax, distant choir]
so here's to when we were gold, to the summer we were gold,
to the brave and stupid children who believed they'd never fold;
wherever you are now, my love, with whoever you call home —
we'll always have that August that has set into our bones,
oh, the summer we were gold... the summer we were gold.

[Outro]
(the groove recedes back to the present-day nylon guitar and lone violin; the photograph is set gently down)
(the summer... we were gold...)
