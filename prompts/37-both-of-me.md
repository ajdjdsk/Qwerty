# Both of Me (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Экспериментальная переработка ядра — неоклассический dark-gothic art-song; скрипка как второй персонаж-«тень», нестабильные размеры (5/4, 7/8) → ровный 4/4, диссонанс → созвучие.

> **Смысл:** Внутренняя гражданская война с самой собой: жестокий внутренний критик против испуганного раненого «я». Героиня годами проигрывает войну, где и победитель, и проигравший носят одно её лицо, — пока от усталости не складывает «нож» и впервые не протягивает руку самой себе: «а что если я не тюремщик и не узник, а обе из меня просто ранены и устали?» Песня о прекращении самобичевания и об интеграции, о перемирии с собой. Близко каждому, кто воевал с собственной головой. Эмоция: тревога и раскол → измождение → нежное примирение и покой.

## Styles (≤ 1000)
Experimental neoclassical dark-gothic chamber-orchestral, a tense art-song reworking of the cinematic core — unstable shifting meters resolving into a steady warm pulse, dissonance into harmony. Warm mature female mezzo-soprano / contralto, intimate close chest voice to a soaring melismatic belt, plus a sharper whispered "shadow" double; sob-catches, glides, breath, hush to wall of sound. The lead solo violin is a second character in continuous duet with the voice — using harmonics, sul ponticello, col legno and weeping glissando for the "shadow self", answering and contradicting then finally harmonizing in unison; deep cello, prepared piano, harp, layered strings, low choir, timpani, pipe organ at the peak. Verses in restless 5/4 and 7/8 unease resolving to a grounded 4/4 climax, minor with chromatic tension into tender major, film-score intensity, rich cathedral reverb, full symphonic production, ~76 BPM. ‑lo-fi, ‑EDM, ‑trap, ‑autotune, ‑male lead, ‑monotone

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(solo violin alone — eerie harmonics and sul ponticello, restless and searching; a single held breath)

[Verse 1 — intimate, uneasy, shifting 7/8 meter; she speaks to the other self]
There's a woman in my mirror who has hated me for years,
she keeps a ledger of my every flaw;
she's the first voice in the morning, she's the last one in my ears,
and I swear she knows me down into the raw.

[Pre-Chorus — strings stir uneasily, cello low]
and I've fought her, oh, I've fought her like an enemy at the gate,
but the blood on both our hands is just the same —

[Chorus — conflicted swell, the violin biting against the voice]
'cause she's both of me, she's both of me,
the cruel one and the small one, and they both are wearing me;
I've been losing every battle in a war I cannot win,
'cause the winner and the loser share one skin —
oh, both of me, both of me,
I am so tired of the fighting... what would happen if I'm free...

[Verse 2 — the shadow answers; sharper whispered double, violin thin and cruel, col legno]
"you were never good enough," she hisses, "and you know it's true,
I only say the things you're scared to hear,"
and the strings go thin and brittle, the way that I taught them to,
every note a way I've made myself disappear.

[Pre-Chorus — building, then exhaustion]
but tonight I am too weary to keep sharpening the knife,
so I lay it down, I lay it down, and breathe —

[Bridge — the turn; meter begins to settle toward 4/4; tender, near-whisper]
what if you're not my jailer, and I'm not your prisoner,
what if the scared and the savage are both just hurting here;
what if I stopped trying to murder all the parts of me I fear,
and I held her, oh, I held her, and I whispered "come here, dear"...

[Violin & Voice Duet]
(the voice and the once-cruel violin stop fighting and slowly begin to align — trading lines, then moving together into unison)

[Final Chorus — grounded warm 4/4, voice and violin in harmony, the truce; full but tender]
'cause she's both of me, she's both of me,
and I'm done dividing armies out of one tired refugee;
let the cruel one and the frightened one lie down and finally rest,
let the long war end — oh, let me be a guest
in my own, my own life — both of me, both of me,
and for once, for once, we're breathing the same breath, and we are free.

[Outro]
(the voice and violin resolve onto a single shared, sustained note, at peace)
(both of me... at last... at peace...)
