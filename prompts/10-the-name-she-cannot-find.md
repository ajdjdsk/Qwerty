# The Name She Cannot Find (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Кинематографичная симфо-баллада, нежно-душераздирающая.

> **Смысл:** Дочь ухаживает за матерью, у которой деменция: мать больше не узнаёт её, путает с чужой, спрашивает «вы из церкви?». Но когда дочь напевает старую колыбельную, пальцы матери сами находят ритм — любовь живёт там, куда уже не достаёт память. Песня о том, что разум отпускает всё, а любовь уходит последней; о роли, когда ребёнок становится родителем своему родителю. Эмоция: щемящая нежность сквозь горе, с проблеском света.

## Lyrics

[Intro — a fragile solo violin plays a tender half-remembered lullaby melody, sparse single piano notes answering, celesta shimmer, almost room silence]

[Verse 1 — whispered, warm, close-mic alto; piano and violin weave gently beneath every phrase, never dropping out]
She looks at me like weather looks at glass,
polite and far away, just waiting for the time to pass,
she asks me, "are you from the church, my dear?"
and I say "yes" — it's softer than the tear.

[Pre-Chorus — cello enters low, strings begin to stir, voice opens slowly from the chest, gliding up]
But when I hum the song she used to sing,
her fingers find the rhythm on the chair,
and oh, for one whole breath she's everything —
the mother who once braided back my hair.

[Chorus — full strings enter in waves, solo violin soaring right alongside the voice, french horn warm; powerful melismatic belt swelling on the long vowels]
She can't find my name, but her hands still know,
they reach for mine the way they did when I was small;
the mind lets go of everything... but love won't go,
love is the last light burning down the hall.
(oh-oh-ohh) so I'll be the name she cannot find,
I'll be the warm she can't explain,
I will love her past the borders of her mind,
and I'll answer to whatever she can name.

[Verse 2 — orchestra eases but piano stays close and conversational under the voice, violin still threading; tender, the roles now reversed]
I learned to fold the world to fit her day,
to button up her coat and look away
the times she's frightened of her own front door —
I am the parent that she had before.

[Pre-Chorus 2 — strings more insistent, french horn early, voice climbing and pouring]
And some grey mornings she is almost here,
she'll touch my face and almost say my name,
then watch it drift like breath upon a mirror —
and still, and still, I love her just the same.

[Chorus — fuller, violin and voice entwined throughout]
She can't find my name, but her hands still know,
they reach for mine the way they did when I was small;
the mind lets go of everything... but love won't go,
love is the last light burning down the hall.
(oh-oh-ohh) so I'll be the name she cannot find,
I'll be the warm she can't explain,
I will love her past the borders of her mind,
and I'll answer to whatever she can name.

[Bridge — sudden hush, only piano and a whispered vocal, long rests; then a single violin note, then strings layering back in]
Last Tuesday, in the lamplight, low and sweet,
she sang two lines of that old lullaby,
and looked at me — and oh, our eyes did meet —
"my baby," she said... "don't you cry, don't cry."
(oh-oh) and I held the woman who once held my whole life,
and I rocked her like she rocked away my fears...

[Final Chorus — maximum, key lifting toward the light, distant choir entering only now, violin wailing above, belt cracking with emotion]
So she can't keep my name, but her heart still knows,
it beats for mine the way it did before my first word at all;
the mind can lose the whole wide world... but love still grows,
love is the last, the last light down the hall.
(oh-oh-ohh) and I'll be the name she cannot find,
I'll be the home she can't explain,
I will love her past the ending of her mind —
and one day, love, I'll find you whole again.

[Outro — strings fall away; piano and violin remain, playing the lullaby from the intro together, then a single warm violin note dissolving as she hums along faintly]
(don't you cry... don't cry...)
