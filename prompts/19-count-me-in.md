# Count Me In (Opus 4.8)

> **Смысл:** Героиня всю жизнь «копила» радость на потом, боясь потратить хоть один счастливый день, и тревожно отсчитывала время. Но часы не замедляются ради осторожных — и она решает не отсчитывать дни вниз, а впустить себя в жизнь целиком. Парадокс финала: когда она перестаёт считать, время будто отпускает её — наступает вечное «сейчас». Смысл: присутствие важнее запаса; жить, а не выживать. Настроение: тревожно-неотвратимое → освобождающе-вне-времени.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a clock ticking, a harpsichord and woodblock pulse, a lone violin slips in over it)

[Verse 1]
I used to count the mornings like a miser counts his gold,
afraid to spend a single shining one,
I kept my joy in jars upon a shelf to save for "old,"
and watched the careful, frightened seasons run.

[Pre-Chorus]
(the tick speeds up, strings begin to climb)
But the ticking doesn't slow for all my saving,
and the jar will shatter empty if I wait —

[Chorus]
So count me in, oh, count me in,
to the dance, to the mess, to the wholly alive,
I am done just keeping time within —
I would rather burn the clock than just survive;
let it tick, let it tick, let it race,
I'm not counting down, I'm counting up the light,
oh, count me in, count me in,
I am spending every second of my life tonight.

[Verse 2]
(faster, more driving, the tick relentless)
I added up my "somedays" and they came to almost none,
I'd been rich in time and poor in being here,
so I'm cracking every jar and pouring gold into the sun,
and I'm laughing at the thing I used to fear.

[Pre-Chorus]
(the tick races, frantic, strings whirling)
'Cause the seconds aren't my jailers, they're my fire,
and I'm striking every one of them alight —

[Bridge]
(the ticking accelerates to a frantic peak — then SUDDENLY STOPS; total silence; the music opens into free rubato, timeless, a long suspended chord, the voice floating)
And then I stopped... and the ticking fell away,
and I felt the strangest thing — the now, the now will stay;
there is no late, there is no soon,
there is only this, this breath, this room.

[Violin Cadenza]
(free, no tempo, soaring and tender)

[Final Chorus]
(senza tempo blooming into a full flowing swell, no rigid tick, choir entering)
So count me in, oh, count me in,
not to the clock, but to the endless, open sky;
I am not the time I'm given, I'm the living,
I'm the part of me that never learned to die;
let it go, let it go, let it cease,
I'm not counting down, I never have to fight —
oh, count me in, count me in,
and the clock, the clock just... let me free tonight.

[Outro]
(one warm sustained note, no ticking, a slow heartbeat fading into stillness)
(count me in... count me in...)
