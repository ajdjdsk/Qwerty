# Three Minutes (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Тёмное аргентинское танго + кинематографичный готик-оркестр (бандонеон, corte-замирания).

> **Смысл:** Бывшие возлюбленные случайно встречаются спустя годы, и одно-единственное танго «за старые времена» вмещает всю жизнь, которую они так и не прожили вместе. Оба знают, что вместе им нельзя — но эти три минуты одного танца принадлежат им целиком. Песня о невозможной любви, о достоинстве зрелого расставания и о праве взять то немногое, что тебе дано, а потом отпустить. Близко каждому, у кого был «тот самый», с кем не сложилось. Эмоция: дымная сдержанная тоска и напряжение → страстная кульминация → холодный, тихий уход.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a brooding bandoneon sigh, a sharp violin glissando, a marcato piano stab, a held dramatic pause — then the habanera pulse drops in)

[Verse 1 — smoky, low, sultry, restrained]
Of all the doorways in this aching city, you walked into mine,
twelve years older, twelve years gone, as cruel and just as kind;
you held your gloved hand out to me like a question I once knew,
and the band, the band struck up the only language left for me and you.

[Pre-Chorus — the pulse tightens, voice leaning in]
We were always so much better dancing than we were in love,
so put your hand against my back, and let us not speak of —

[Chorus — sultry belt, violin and bandoneon swelling with the voice]
three minutes — give me three minutes,
the whole long life we never got to live,
let the bandoneon decide it, let me be the one you hold
for as long as one slow tango has to give;
I won't ask you for forever, I have learned what we are not,
just don't you let me go before the final note —
three minutes, oh, three minutes, then we let the lights go cold.

[Corte — sudden full stop; only breath and a heartbeat in the silence]

[Verse 2 — fuller, marcato strings biting]
You still lead like you're certain, and I still follow like a fool,
we trace the same old wounds across this candle-bitten floor;
your wedding ring is colder than the air between us two,
but oh, the body remembers what the mind has locked the door —

[Pre-Chorus — building, accelerating]
so let the music lie to us for one more turning round,
let the strings pretend we found the life we never found —

[Bridge — a freeze, then a raw confession that surges]
(the dance halts; bare voice, trembling)
I'm not asking you to stay, I'm not asking you to choose,
I know the morning takes you, and I know what I will lose;
just give me this — this single, spinning, borrowed little while —
(the pulse crashes back) — and I'll carry it for years inside a smile...

[Violin & Bandoneon Duel]
(the violin and bandoneon chase each other, fierce and weeping, accelerating to the peak)

[Final Chorus — key lift, dramatic, the dance at its most passionate]
So three minutes — we had three minutes,
the only honest thing we'll ever do,
let the whole hall fall around us, let me memorize your hands,
let me have the life I lost in losing you;
I will not beg you for tomorrow, I have made my peace with no,
just hold me, hold me, hold me till the final, final note —
three minutes, oh, three minutes... then I turn, and let you go.

[Outro]
(the music stops dead on the last note; footsteps walking away across an empty floor; a single lonesome bandoneon, then silence)
(three minutes... then I let you go...)
