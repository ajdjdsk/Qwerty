# The Wolves at the Gate (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Эпик battle-orchestral, героический и трагически-славный.

> **Смысл:** Горстка защитников встречает несметную осаду — без надежды победить, но чтобы выиграть время для тех, кто за их спинами (детей, стариков, целой деревни) успел уйти. Песня о тех, кто сознательно «держит стену», зная цену; о мужестве и любви, которые сильнее страха смерти. «Мы — стена перед стеной». Эмоция: яростно-героическая, трагически-славная; динамика через край.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a distant war-horn, a low choir chant "hah!", thunderous taiko hits, then a fierce violin ostinato bursts in)

[Verse 1]
They counted us at forty, counted them ten thousand strong,
their banners on the hillside black as crows;
my mother's sword is heavy and the winter night is long,
but behind us sleep the children, and the road by which they go —
everyone we ever swore that we would keep,
so let them come, let them come, while the little ones still sleep.

[Pre-Chorus]
And I'm not here to win this, love, I'm here to make them pay
for every stolen hour, buy our people one more day —

[Chorus]
So let the wolves come to the gate,
they'll find us standing, find us tall,
we are the ones who hold the weight,
we are the wall before the wall —
sound the horn, oh, sound the horn,
let them learn the cost of dawn;
we may not see the morning born,
but oh, the morning carries on.

[Verse 2]
So tie the gates with everything our trembling hands can lift,
pour the oil, light the pitch, and take your mark;
I have made my peace with God and every grudge I couldn't shift,
and I'll carry every name I love like fire through the dark.
they will write us small, a footnote in the snow —
but the ones we held the line for, oh, the ones we held will grow.

[Pre-Chorus]
And if my blood's the price of one more sunrise on their faces,
then take it, take it, take it — I will hold a thousand places —

[Chorus]
So let the wolves come to the gate,
they'll find us standing, find us tall,
we are the ones who hold the weight,
we are the wall before the wall —
sound the horn, oh, sound the horn,
let them learn the cost of dawn;
we may not see the morning born,
but oh, the morning carries on.

[Bridge]
(sudden hush — a whispered vow over a single low drum, then the build returns)
If you make it past the mountains, if you find a kinder shore,
don't you weep for us, don't you carry what we bore —
just live so loud, so wide, so unafraid,
that they'll know exactly what it was we paid...
(oh-oh) now raise the banner, raise it, let it climb —
we are not afraid, we have run out of time —

[Violin & War-Drum Charge]
(the ostinato erupts, taikos pounding, brass and choir surging into the charge)

[Final Chorus]
(colossal, full choir and organ, belt at the absolute peak)
So the wolves have come to the gate,
and they found us blazing, found us whole,
we were the ones who held the weight,
we were the wall, we were the soul —
sound the horn, oh, sound the horn,
the sun is climbing, red and warm;
we did not see the morning born,
but oh — the morning carries on.

[Outro]
(the storm of battle thins to a lone violin over a distant, fading choir, one long held note like dawn over the silent field)
(the morning... carries on...)
