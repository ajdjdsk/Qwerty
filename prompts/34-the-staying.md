# The Staying (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Готик-симфоническая кинематографичная баллада (нежная, dark romantic) — ядровая эстетика.

> **Смысл:** Гимн негромкому героизму долгой любви длиною в жизнь — тому, о чём почти не пишут песен. Не про искры и первый поцелуй, а про сорок тысяч ужинов, холодные ступни, что находят твои под одеялом, и про тысячи скучных вторников; про то, как остаться — в сером, в обыденном, в трудном. В финале — поворот: он стар и угасает, а она у его постели утверждает, что прожила бы каждый тусклый и золотой день снова. Тема близка каждому, кто любит долго и по-настоящему. Эмоция: тихая нежность → сокрушительная благодарность → тёплый покой.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a faint kitchen-clock tick, a glowing piano figure, a lone tender violin; very intimate, warm)

[Verse 1 — tender breathy chest voice, intimate, just piano and violin]
It wasn't ever fireworks, it was a kettle every dawn,
it was your cold feet finding mine before the words came on,
it was forty thousand dinners and a quiet kind of warming,
it was every boring Tuesday that nobody wrote down.

[Pre-Chorus — strings stir, voice lifting]
And they sing about the lightning, about the first and frantic kiss,
but nobody, oh, nobody tells you that the love, the love is this —

[Chorus — soaring warm belt, orchestra opening]
they'll write you songs about the falling, all the falling, love,
but here's a song about the staying, about the staying on;
through the grey and through the ordinary, through the rooms we grew to be,
oh, the bravest thing I ever did was stay — was stay with you, with me;
so this is for the staying, love, this is for the staying.

[Verse 2 — fuller, cello and harp enter]
There were years we barely liked each other, years the money ran,
there was one small grave in springtime we still cannot understand,
there were doors I nearly walked through, there were nights I packed a case —
and then your tired, stubborn, gentle hand would find me in the dark.

[Pre-Chorus — building, french horn]
'Cause anyone can love the easy, love the golden and the new,
but I learned to love the weather, all the weather, loving you —

[Chorus — bigger]
they'll write you songs about the falling, all the falling, love,
but here's a song about the staying, about the staying on;
through the grey and through the ordinary, through the rooms we grew to be,
oh, the bravest thing I ever did was stay — was stay with you, with me;
so this is for the staying, love, this is for the staying.

[Bridge — the turn; near-whisper at his bedside, then rising]
And now your hands are paper and your memory comes and goes,
and you ask me am I married, with a shy and crooked smile,
and I say "fifty years this autumn, love" — and watch the wonder rose —
oh, I'd marry you tomorrow, and I'd do the whole long while...

[Violin Cadenza]
(the violin lifts and soars, gathering a lifetime of ordinary days into one cresting wave)

[Final Chorus — key lift, full tender orchestra and distant choir, belt with tears]
So sing your songs about the falling, all the falling, love,
but I will take the staying, oh, the staying, every time;
every dull and golden morning, every kettle, every chair,
I would live it all again with you — again, again, I swear;
this was always for the staying, love... this was for the staying.

[Outro]
(everything falls away to the kitchen clock, the lone piano and one warm held violin note)
(this was for the staying... love...)
