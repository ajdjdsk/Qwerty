# Whenever You Come Home (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Доработанное ядро — готик-симфоническая кинематографичная баллада (тёплая, dark romantic), женский голос. Приём: непрерывный мотив-«свет», что не гаснет через весь трек.

> **Смысл:** Мать, чей взрослый ребёнок порвал связь, потерялся или ушёл (оставлено открыто — ссора, зависимость, расстояние), каждую ночь оставляет свет на крыльце и дверь незапертой, хранит его комнату нетронутой — не из отрицания, а как безусловное, не ведущее счёт приглашение: «без вопросов и упрёков — просто вернись, каким угодно и когда угодно». О терпеливой родительской любви, прощении без условий и о свете, что не гаснет, даже если в дверь никто так и не войдёт. Близко всем, кто кого-то ждёт или потерял связь с родными. Эмоция: тихая боль ожидания → яростная безусловная нежность → открытая, незатухающая надежда.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a warm sustained celesta-and-violin "light" glow; a quiet dark house, a ticking clock, the hush of a porch at night)

[Verse 1 — quiet, tender, the empty house]
The third stair still creaks where you used to sneak in late,
your coffee mug is hanging on its hook;
I keep your room exactly, and I don't touch it, and I wait,
and every single night I leave the porch light on — and look.

[Pre-Chorus — strings stir, the glow steady beneath]
I don't know what I broke, love, or what you couldn't say,
but none of it, oh none of it, could keep me from this day —

[Chorus — soaring warm belt, the light motif glowing through]
so whenever you come home, whenever you come home,
there's no questions and no blaming at my door;
be it midnight, be it ruin, be it years I've never known,
come exactly as you're broken, and you'll never be ignored —
oh, the light is always burning, and you'll never be alone,
the door is never locked, my love — whenever you come home.

[Verse 2 — the ache, the years, the not-knowing; fuller, cello and harp]
some nights I don't know if you're breathing, if you're warm,
I read the names in papers that I pray I'll never find;
but I set a second place each night and I weather every storm,
'cause a mother's door stays open even when she's lost her mind — to worry.

[Pre-Chorus — building, french horn]
they tell me let you go now, that it's healthier, it's wise,
but they have never seen your face, they've never seen your eyes —

[Bridge — fierce unconditional love; even if never; the light is who she is]
and maybe you won't come, love — maybe this is all there'll be:
an old woman and a porch light and a door she'll never close;
but I'd rather keep it burning into all eternity
than be the kind of love that locks, that bargains, or that goes —
so the light stays on, the light stays on —

[Violin & Orchestra Lift]
(the violin and full orchestra rise warm around the never-dying glow)

[Final Chorus — key lift, open-armed, radiant, belt with tears]
so whenever you come home, whenever you come home —
this year, or in a decade, or with grey in all your hair —
you will find the kettle waiting and the same light through the loam,
and a pair of arms that never, ever stopped being right there;
oh, I am not the keeping-score, I'm the candle and the comb,
I will love you from this window — whenever you come home.

[Outro]
(the light glows on; the clock ticks; then a soft, uncertain knock at the door... or only the wind — and the violin holds one warm, hopeful note)
(whenever you come home... I'm right here...)
