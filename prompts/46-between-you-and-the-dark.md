# Between You and the Dark (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** ЭКСПЕРИМЕНТ С МУЖСКИМ ГОЛОСОМ (временно) — чистое готик-симфо ЯДРО (оркестр + скрипка, без электрогитар), но с мощным гриттовым анфемным мужским вокалом (в духе того голоса, что тебе нравится; исполнитель не упоминается).

> **Смысл:** Мужчина даёт ночной обет быть стеной между всем, что он любит, и всей тьмой мира — забирать на себя удары, страхи и волков, что ходят у края их света, чтобы его близкие могли спать спокойно и никогда не узнать, чего это стоит. В бридже — трещина: он тоже устал и боится, но именно поэтому стоит. Песня о защитной любви и тихой жертве тех, кто «держит вахту»; о героизме не как славе, а как любви. Близко родителям, защитникам, всем, кто прикрывает других собой. Эмоция: интимный обет → героический подъём → нежная, несокрушимая клятва.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a lone violin and piano, a low cello of oncoming dread; a vow beginning to form)

[Verse 1 — intimate, controlled power, warm and low]
You don't have to see the things I see when you're asleep,
the wolves that pace the edges of our light;
I strike a bargain nightly with the dark that I will keep
every promise I am strong enough to fight.

[Pre-Chorus — strings stir, timpani heartbeat, voice rising]
so close your eyes, my love, I've got the watch tonight,
let me carry what you never have to know —

[Chorus — soaring heroic belt with grit, violin in call-and-response]
I will be the wall between you and the dark,
the thing it has to break before it touches you;
let it come for me, let it aim it at my heart,
I was built for this, my love — this is what I'm here to do;
oh, I'll stand, I will stand, though it burns me to the spark,
I will be the wall between you and the dark.

[Verse 2 — fuller, the cost showing, cello and horns deepening]
I have taken blows you'll never know were aimed your way,
I have swallowed every fear so you could dream;
and I'd carry it a thousand lives, a thousand nights, a thousand days,
'cause your laughter is the only church I keep.

[Pre-Chorus — building hard, brass and choir]
so don't you worry over shadows on the door,
that's just me out in the dark, doing what I'm for —

[Bridge — the crack in the armor; near-whisper, then a rising vow]
and yes, some nights I'm frightened, and some nights I am tired,
some nights the dark is bigger than the man;
but I look at you still sleeping, and the failing flame's re-fired,
and I stand back up and hold the only line I can —

[Violin & Orchestra Heroic Lift]
(the violin and full orchestra surge, gathering the vow into a towering wave)

[Final Chorus — key lift, colossal, choir and organ, belt cracking with love]
so I will be the wall between you and the dark,
and it will not, it will not ever get to you;
let it howl my name, let it tear me apart,
I'd be broken a thousand times if a thousand times would do;
oh, I'll stand, I will stand, I'm the keeper of your spark,
I will be the wall, I'll be the wall between you and the dark.

[Outro]
(the orchestra settles to the lone violin and piano; a steady, tender breath in the quiet watch)
(go to sleep now... I've got the dark...)
