# Vengeance Is a Patient Beast (Opus 4.8)

> **Смысл:** Женщину когда-то сломали и забыли — а она годами тихо «вела учёт», оттачивая холодную решимость. Теперь она приходит за расплатой. Это не горячая вспышка, а терпеливый хищник, который ждал. В бридже — отрезвляющая правда: месть тоже пожирает того, кто её несёт, но она всё равно делает выбор. Песня о долготерпении обиженных и о цене справедливости. Эмоция: змеино-холодная угроза, нарастающая до взрывной расплаты; динамика через край.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a low stalking cello and contrabass groove, a single high violin harmonic, a distant whisper, menace coiling and building)

[Verse 1]
You forgot my face the moment that you turned the key,
you slept like men who never count the cost,
but a debt does not dissolve 'cause you don't remember me —
oh, I kept the ledger, darling, of the everything I lost.
I have been so patient, I have been so still,
sharpening the silence, biding for the kill.

[Pre-Chorus]
And the years have only honed it, made it colder, made it clean,
I'm not the girl you broke — I am the reckoning between —

[Chorus]
'Cause vengeance is a patient beast,
it waits and waits and learns your name,
it lets you laugh, it lets you feast,
then it arrives like falling flame —
run, run while you still can,
there's a storm where mercy used to be;
vengeance is a patient beast,
and oh, my love — it's coming, it is me.

[Verse 2]
I rebuilt myself in shadow out of everything you burned,
I learned the patient languages of stone,
every lock you ever shut me out of, now I've learned —
and the house you thought was safe was never yours alone.
so keep on sleeping, keep your easy, guiltless rest,
while I climb the stairs so quiet toward the debt.

[Pre-Chorus]

[Bridge]
(sudden drop to near-silence, a sober whisper over a lone violin)
But I felt it at the threshold, the cold beneath the win:
the beast I starved to send for you has hollowed me within.
do I open it, or turn — and let the ledger fall?
(the drum begins again, low and certain) ...
no. I have carried this too far, too long, through all —
so I square my shoulders, and I answer to the call.

[Violin Cadenza & Storm Build]
(blistering runs climbing, taikos and brass crashing in one by one, the eruption gathering)

[Final Chorus]
(maximum release, full orchestra, choir, belt at the peak)
'Cause vengeance is a patient beast,
and tonight the patient beast is fed,
I am the famine and the feast,
I am the words you should have said —
no more running, no more lies,
there's a fire where your mercy used to be;
vengeance is a patient beast,
and oh, my love — it found you, it is me.

[Outro]
(the storm exhales into a cold, ringing quiet, the lone violin holds one final low note, then silence)
(it is me... it is me...)
