# Everything I Never Said (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Кинематографичная симфо-баллада, исповедальная (виолончель-дуэт).

> **Смысл:** Все слова, которые мы так и не сказали тому, кого потеряли (или кто отдалился): «останься», «прости», «я люблю тебя» — сложенные и спрятанные, как камни под кроватью, ради разговора, которого уже не будет. Героиня наконец произносит их в пустоту — в закат, в тишину — и это одновременно горе и освобождение. Песня-предупреждение: не оставляй любовь невысказанной. Эмоция: исповедальная, с сильными эмоциональными приливами и отливами (динамика, не боевая). Виолончель отвечает голосу как «второй собеседник».

## Lyrics

[Intro]

[Verse 1]
I rehearse it in the kitchen to the steam,
all the sentences I folded up and hid,
how I loved you in the safety of a dream
but I never, never said it like I did.

[Pre-Chorus]
And the kettle keeps on crying like it knows,
oh, the things we save are the things we never chose —

[Chorus]
So here is everything I never said,
every "stay," every "sorry," every "don't,"
all the words I kept like stones beneath my bed
for a someday-conversation that we won't.
oh, I'd give my careful silence, all of it,
for one reckless, trembling, spoken thing —
so I'm saying it now where the light has lit:
I loved you, oh, I loved you — let it ring.

[Verse 2]
You'd think the brave part's living, but it's this —
the saying of the soft and naked true,
the un-swallowed apology, the kiss
I held behind my teeth instead of you.

[Pre-Chorus]
And the years are quiet rooms I can't go back in,
but I'll fling these windows open, let the whole sky in —

[Chorus]
So here is everything I never said,
every "stay," every "sorry," every "don't,"
all the words I kept like stones beneath my bed
for a someday-conversation that we won't.
oh, I'd give my careful silence, all of it,
for one reckless, trembling, spoken thing —
so I'm saying it now where the light has lit:
I loved you, oh, I loved you — let it ring.

[Bridge]
(near-silence, voice and cello alone, in duet)
If you can hear me from wherever quiet is,
let the wind report it, let it carry on:
I was never cold, I was only scared like this —
and the love, oh, the love was never gone.

[Violin & Cello Duet]

[Final Chorus]
(key change, full orchestra, choir entering)
So here is everything I never said,
shouted finally to the dark and to the dawn,
all the love I rationed, all the love I fed
to a silence — here, now, loud, and never gone.
oh, don't you wait, don't ration what you feel,
don't save the saying for a bell that may not ring —
say it reckless, say it trembling, say it real:
"I love you" — oh, "I love you" — let it ring.

[Outro]
(strings fall away, cello holds one warm low note, a final whisper)
(I loved you... let it ring...)
