# I Burned the Way Back (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Эпик готик battle-orchestral, драйвовый и дерзкий.

> **Смысл:** Женщина уходит из жизни, которая её уменьшала — токсичных отношений, навязанной роли, испуганной версии себя — и сознательно «сжигает дорогу назад», чтобы не было соблазна приползти обратно к привычной боли. Это страшно и освобождающе одновременно: никакого возврата, только неизвестность впереди. Песня о радикальной решимости меняться, о том, что иногда спасение — это отрезать путь к отступлению. Эмоция: контролируемая ярость → яростная свобода. Драйв с первой секунды.

## Lyrics

[Intro — a hard war-drum stomp hits immediately under a fierce sawing violin ostinato and a low choir chant; a wordless "oh-oh-ohh" cuts over it; gripping and urgent from the first second]

[Verse 1 — low, controlled fury, close alto; the drum pulse and violin ostinato keep driving beneath every line]
I packed the silent years into a single flame,
every "stay," every "you'll never make it on your own,"
every door I begged at, every time I shrank to fit the frame,
every version of me that learned to live like stone.
I stood out on the road I crawled a thousand times,
the one that always led me gently back to less —
and I struck the match against my own old frightened rhymes,
and I watched it take the bridge, and I said yes.

[Pre-Chorus — strings climb with the violin, timpani builds, voice rising sharp]
'Cause the smoke is rising sweet behind me,
and my begging hands have finally stopped —
there's no road home, I made sure of it,
I burned it down — every step, every stop.

[Chorus — full battle-orchestra explodes, taikos and brass, violin soaring with the voice throughout; commanding melismatic belt]
I burned the way back, let it fall,
there's no crawling home to who I was;
I will not unbecome it — no, not at all,
I'd face the howling wild before those walls, because —
(oh-oh-ohh) the girl who stayed would not survive,
so I lit the bridge and I let her go;
I burned the way back just to stay alive,
and the only road is the one I do not know.

[Verse 2 — heavier stomp, brass stabs answer each line, relentless]
Oh, they'll call me cold, they'll call me hard to love,
they always do when a quiet woman finally roars;
but I have spent my softest years in service of
the comfort of the people behind those closing doors.
I am not the wreckage — I'm the one who walked away
while the embers wrote my name across the dark;
let them keep the ashes, let them keep the day —
I am taking back the fire, I am taking back the spark.

[Pre-Chorus 2 — massive, organ and choir build, drums double-time push]
'Cause the smoke is rising holy now,
and the past has nothing left to hold —
there's no road home, I'm certain of it,
I burned it down, and I have never been so bold.

[Chorus — even bigger, full choir chant under the belt]
I burned the way back, let it fall,
there's no crawling home to who I was;
I will not unbecome it — no, not at all,
I'd face the howling wild before those walls, because —
(oh-oh-ohh) the girl who stayed would not survive,
so I lit the bridge and I let her go;
I burned the way back just to stay alive,
and the only road is the one I do not know.

[Bridge — sudden drop to near-silence; a lone fierce violin cadenza tears upward, then a low trembling vocal admits the fear before the drums crash back one hit at a time]
And I won't pretend I am not afraid —
the dark ahead has no familiar shape,
I traded every cage I ever made
for the terrifying, open, unmapped escape.
(oh-oh) but I would rather burn alive and free
than be warm and slowly vanish in that room —
so let it blaze, let it light the road for me,
let the ending of the old be the new bloom.

[Final Chorus — colossal, key lifted, full choir and organ, violin wailing above everything, belt at full liberated power]
So I burned the way back, watched it fall,
and I will not be returning, not at all;
I am everything they swore I'd never be —
I'm the fire and the forest and the free, because —
(oh-oh-ohh) the woman that I am will now survive,
I lit the bridge and let the old me go;
I burned the way back, and I'm so alive,
and the only road is the one I'll come to know.

[Outro — the stomp slows to a proud march, the violin ostinato plays one last fierce time, a single held warm vocal note rings out, then one cymbal swell and sudden silence]
(the only road... is the one... I'll come to know...)
