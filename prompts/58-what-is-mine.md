# What Is Mine (Opus 4.8)

> **Направление / стиль:** Мощное ядро на максимум — эпик готик-симфо анфем, первобытный и стихийный; приём «обманчиво нежное начало → монументальное, почти звериное извержение» (мягкость как лицо неостановимой силы).

> **Смысл:** Обычно нежная мать оборачивается стихийной, ужасающей силой, когда угрожают её ребёнку: «вы видели меня только мягкой стороной наружу и приняли нежность за слабость». Глубина в том, что эта пугающая мощь и есть самая абсолютная любовь — она перекроит мир ради своего. Песня о материнской защитной силе, самой первобытной, что есть, и о том, что мягкость бывает лицом неудержимой стихии. Близко любому родителю и защитнику. Эмоция: колыбельная нежность → нарастающее предупреждение → монументальная, стихийная ярость любви → возвращение к нежности (дитя в безопасности), но сила всё ещё рядом.

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(a soft lullaby hum, a gentle violin and a music-box, tender and calm; a low primal drone barely breathing underneath)

[Verse 1 — tender, soft, the gentle mother, almost a lullaby]
You have only ever seen me with the soft side facing out,
the lullabies, the kissing of the knee;
you think you know my nature, think there's nothing here to doubt,
'cause I'm gentle as the morning, mild as tea.

[Pre-Chorus — the drone rising, a low warning, the air changing]
but you turned your hand toward the little life I grew,
and something ancient woke that you have never, never knew —

[Chorus — the softness transforms into elemental power, belt with a feral edge, full orchestra]
'cause what is mine, what is mine, you will never, never touch,
I am softer than the lamb — until you reach for what I love;
then I'm the flood, I'm the famine, I'm the mountain, I'm too much,
I'm the thing your nightmares pray to, I'm the earth and storm above —
oh, what is mine, what is mine — you mistook my tender for the tame,
now the gentlest hands you ever knew are wrist-deep in the flame.

[Verse 2 — fuller, elemental, war-drums creeping, the she-wolf rising]
they wrote the oldest stories wrong, they made us out as weak,
the soft ones with the babies and the bread;
but ask the wolf, the bear, the tide, the thing that will not speak —
there's nothing on this earth like what a mother will when cornered, when she's led
to the edge of all her loving —

[Pre-Chorus — massive, choir and drums building]
so you can have my mercy, you can have my open door,
but the one thing in creation that I'll end the world to keep —

[Bridge — the depth: this power IS the love; tender and terrifying at once]
hear me gentle, hear me clearly, 'cause I'll only say it once:
this is not my rage, my darling — this is love without its leash;
I would unmake every heaven, I would drown the whole response,
I would burn through my own marrow just to grant my child some peace —
(the drums crash) — so do not, do not test the quiet woman in the door —

[Violin & War-Drum Eruption]
(the violin screams over thunderous war-drums and full choir — the elemental force at its absolute peak)

[Final Chorus — monumental, key lifted, feral belt and choir blazing]
'cause what is mine, what is mine, you will never, never have,
I am the softest thing you'll ever meet and the last thing you'll provoke;
I'm the cradle and the cataclysm, I'm the balm and I'm the wrath,
I'm the love that built a universe and the love that lets it choke —
oh, what is mine, what is mine — let the whole wide world take aim,
they will meet a tender mountain, and they'll learn, they'll learn my name.

[Outro]
(the storm recedes to the soft lullaby hum, the gentle violin and music-box; the child is safe — but the low primal drone goes on humming, never far)
(hush now... what is mine... is safe... hush now...)
